Select the correct answer using the code give-n below:

b) 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 only

c) 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 only

d) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 only

**Explanation:**
- If a major solar storm (solar flare) reaches Earth, the following are the possible effects.

- 1. GPS and navigation systems could fail: **True.** Solar storms can interfere with satellite signals, disrupting GPS and other navigation systems.

- 2. Tsunamis could occur at equatorial regions: **False.** Solar storms do not cause tsunamis. Tsunamis are caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, or other geological activities.

- 3. Power grids could be damaged: **True.** Solar storms can induce geomagnetic currents that overload and damage power grids, potentially causing widespread power outages.

- 4. Intense auroras could occur over much of the Earth: **True.** Solar storms can cause intense auroras, extending their visibility to regions much farther from the poles than usual.

- 5. Forest fires could take place over much of the planet: **False.** Solar storms do not cause forest fires. Forest fires are caused by factors such as lightning, human activities, and dry conditions.

- 6. Orbits of the satellites could be disturbed: **True.** Solar storms can increase atmospheric drag on satellites, potentially altering their orbits.

- 7. Shortwave radio communication of the aircraft flying over polar regions could be interrupted: **True**. Solar storms can disrupt high-frequency radio communications, particularly in polar regions where the Earth’s magnetic field lines converge.

c) 10.00 p.m

**Answer:**

**Explanation:**
- Given that moon takes 30 days to complete the cycle.

- After complete cycle (30 days), moon will rise again at 6.48 pm (completing 24 hrs cycle).

- Since moon travels around earth from west to east, every day, it rises slightly later.

- Since moon has to complete 24 hr cycle in 30 days, in a day it will complete 24hr/30 = 0.8hr

- This implies that every next day, moon will rise 0.8 hr later = 0.8 x 60 = 48 minutes

- To calculate time on 4th day, we have to add 3 days’ worth of time (1-2, 2-3, and 3-4th day) = 3 x 48 = 144 mins = 2 hr 24 minutes

- If we add 2hr 24 minutes to 6.48pm, we will get our answer

- 6:48 + 2:24 = 09:12 pm

- Thus, 
b) is correct answer.
