### Question 11
**1] For the measurement/estimation of which of the following are satellite images/remote sensing data used? [2019]
**
1. Chlorophyll content in the vegetation of a specific location
2. Greenhouse gas emissions from rice paddies of a specific location
3. Land surface temperatures of a specific location

Select the correct answer using the code given below.

a) 1 Only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- All three can be done.

- **Chlorophyll content in the vegetation** can be estimated using remote sensing techniques such as multispectral or hyperspectral imaging. These methods involve capturing images of the Earth’s surface at different wavelengths of light, including those absorbed and reflected by chlorophyll in vegetation. By analysing the spectral signatures of vegetation, scientists can infer the chlorophyll content and assess the health and productivity of plant life.

- To measure** Greenhouse gas emissions from rice paddies, **satellite imagery can be used to detect changes in land cover and land use, including the flooding and drying cycles of rice paddies, which influence methane emissions.

- Remote sensing satellites equipped with thermal infrared sensors are used to measure **land surface temperatures.** These sensors detect the thermal radiation emitted by the Earth’s surface in the form of heat. This information is crucial for understanding climate patterns, urban heat islands, agricultural practices, and environmental changes.
