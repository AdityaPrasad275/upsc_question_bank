### Question 13
**1] In which of the following area can GPS technology be used? [2018]
**
1. Mobile phones operation
2. Banking operations
3. Controlling the power grids

Which of the pairs given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- GPS can (in facts it’s already in use) be used in all the three places given.

- The use of GPS in mobile phones is well known.

- Regarding banking operations, GPS nowadays is used on payments apps, to locate ATM machines, POS machines etc.

- GPS is also used in power grid control systems to synchronize and coordinate the operation of electricity generation, transmission, and distribution facilities across wide geographic areas. By accurately timestamping data and synchronizing the timing of grid operations with GPS signals, power grid operators can monitor and control the flow of electricity more efficiently, optimize grid performance, and respond to grid disturbances and outages in real-time.
