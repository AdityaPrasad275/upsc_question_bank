### Question 14
**2] Consider the following statements: [2018]
**
1. The Earth’s magnetic fields has reserved every few hundred thousand years.
2. When the Earth was created more than 4000 millions years ago, there was 54% oxygen and no carbon dioxide.
3. When living organism originated, they modified the early atmosphere of the Earth.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Earth’s magnetic field reverses due to changes in the movement of molten iron and nickel in the outer core of the planet. This process is called geomagnetic reversal or magnetic field reversal. The frequency of these reversals is highly variable and irregular, with intervals ranging from tens of thousands to millions of years. The last reversal, known as the Brunhes-Matuyama reversal, occurred approximately 780,000 years ago. **Thus, first statement is correct.**

- According to prevalent theory, the formation of Earth occurred approximately 4.5 billion (4500 million) years ago. The early atmosphere of Earth is believed to have been primarily composed of gases such as hydrogen, helium, methane, ammonia, and water vapor. **The oxygen was particularly absent (present in insignificant amount).** This atmosphere was formed through processes such as outgassing from volcanic activity, impact degassing from meteorite bombardment, and chemical reactions on the Earth’s surface. **Hence, 2nd statement is not correct.**

- The current theory about formation of earth suggests that early organisms on Earth, primarily cyanobacteria, played a crucial role in shaping the planet’s atmosphere. Cyanobacteria were among the first organisms to perform oxygenic photosynthesis, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. Over billions of years, the accumulation of oxygen in the atmosphere led to the Great Oxidation Event, around 2.4 billion years ago, which significantly increased atmospheric oxygen levels. This oxygenation of the atmosphere had profound effects on the evolution of life and the chemistry of the Earth’s surface, leading to the development of aerobic organisms and ozone layer formation, which protected life from harmful ultraviolet radiation. **Thus, statement 3 is also correct.**
