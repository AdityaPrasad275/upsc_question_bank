### Question 18
**1] The terms ‘Event Horizon’, ‘Singularity’, `String Theory’ and ‘Standard Model’ are sometimes seen in the news in the context of: [2017]
**

a) Observation and understanding of the Universe

b) Study of the solar and the lunar eclipses

c) Placing satellites in the orbit of the Earth

d) Origin and evolution of living organisms on the Earth

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Observation and understanding of the Universe

**Explanation:**
- Event Horizon: This refers to the boundary around a black hole beyond which no information or matter can escape. It’s the point at which the gravitational pull becomes so strong that not even light can escape.

- Singularity: This is a point in space-time where gravitational forces cause matter to have an infinite density and zero volume. Singularities are found at the centers of black holes.

- String Theory: This is a theoretical framework in which the point-like particles of particle physics are replaced by one-dimensional objects called “strings.” It aims to reconcile quantum mechanics and general relativity, potentially providing a unified description of all fundamental forces and forms of matter.

- Standard Model: This is a theory in particle physics that describes the electromagnetic, weak, and strong nuclear interactions. It classifies all known subatomic particles and explains how they interact through fundamental forces.

- Thus, a is correct answer.
