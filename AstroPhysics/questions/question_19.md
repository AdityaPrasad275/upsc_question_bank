### Question 19
**2] What is the purpose of ‘evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (eLIS
A)’ project? [2017]
**

a) To detect neutrinos

b) To detect gravitational waves

c) To detect the effectiveness of missile defence system

d) To study the effect of solar flares on our communication systems

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) To detect gravitational waves

**Explanation:**
- The purpose of the Evolved Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (eLIS
A) project is to **detect and study gravitational waves** with unprecedented sensitivity and accuracy.

- Building upon the success of the Laser Interferometer Space Antenna (LIS
A) Pathfinder mission, eLISA aims to create a space-based gravitational wave observatory capable of detecting low-frequency gravitational waves with wavelengths ranging from 1 million to 100 million kilometers.

- By observing these waves, eLISA seeks to unlock new insights into astrophysical phenomena such as black hole mergers, binary star systems, and the early universe. The project aims to enhance our understanding of gravity and the structure of the universe, opening new avenues for exploring the cosmos.

- The launch of the three spacecraft is planned for 2035.

- Thus, answer b is correct.
