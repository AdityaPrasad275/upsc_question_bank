### Question 27
**2] Electrically charged particles from space travelling at speeds of several hundred km/sec can severely harm living beings if they reach the surface of the Earth. What prevents them from reaching the surface of the Earth? [2012]**


a) The Earth’s magnetic field diverts them towards its poles

b) Ozone layer around the Earth reflects them back to outer space

c) Moisture in the upper layers of atmosphere prevents them from reaching the surface of the Earth

d) None of the statements (
a), (
b) and (e) given above is correct

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) The Earth’s magnetic field diverts them towards its poles

**Explanation:**
- The Earth’s magnetic field acts as a protective shield against electrically charged particles from space. This magnetic field deflects most of the charged particles away from the Earth’s surface, guiding them along the planet’s magnetic field lines. This phenomenon is often referred to as the “magnetosphere.” Additionally, the Earth’s atmosphere absorbs and scatters many of the remaining charged particles, further reducing their impact on the surface.

- Thus, option 
a) is correct answer.
