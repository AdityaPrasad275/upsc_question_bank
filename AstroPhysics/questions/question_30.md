### Question 30
**3] What is difference between asteroids and comets? [2011]**

1. Asteroids are small rocky planetoids, while comets are formed of frozen gases held together by rocky and metallic material.
2. Asteroids are found mostly between the orbits of Jupiter and mars, while comets are found mostly between Venus
and mercury.
3. Comets show a perceptible glowing tail, while asteroids do not.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 and 2 only 
b) 1 and 3 only

c) 3 only 
d) 1,2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Differences between asteroids and comets are:

- **Composition:** Asteroids are primarily composed of rocky and metallic materials, similar to the composition of planets. Comets, on the other hand, consist of ice, dust, and rocky material, often referred to as “dirty snowballs.”

- **Appearance:** Asteroids typically have a rocky appearance and may have irregular shapes. Comets often have a coma, which is a cloud of gas and dust surrounding their nucleus, and a tail that forms as they approach the Sun due to the sublimation of ice.

- **Orbits:** Asteroids generally orbit the Sun in the asteroid belt located between Mars and Jupiter, although some can be found elsewhere in the solar system. Comets usually have highly elliptical orbits that can take them far from the Sun into the outer reaches of the solar system, but they often swing close to the Sun during their journey.

- **Origin:** Asteroids are believed to be remnants from the early solar system, leftover material that never coalesced into planets. Comets are thought to originate from the Kuiper Belt and the Oort Cloud, regions beyond the orbit of Neptune where icy bodies reside.

- **Thus, statements 1 and 3 are correct.**
