### Question 31
**1] In the context of space technology, what is “Bhuvan”, recently in the news? [2010]**


a) A mini satellite launched by ISRO for promoting the distance education in India.

b) The name given to the next moon Impact Probe, for Chandrayan – II.

c) A geoportal of ISRO with 3D imaging capabilities of India.

d) A space telescope developed by India.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) A geoportal of ISRO with 3D imaging capabilities of India.

**Explanation:**
- In 2010, “Bhuvan” was in the news as an Indian satellite mapping tool developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

- Bhuvan is a web-based geospatial platform that provides satellite imagery and mapping services for various applications, including urban planning, disaster management, agriculture, and environmental monitoring.

- It allows users to view and analyse high-resolution satellite images of India and supports features such as **3D visualization**, thematic mapping, and data integration. Bhuvan was significant because it aimed to provide indigenous satellite mapping capabilities to support various sectors in India.

- Thus, 
c) is the correct answer.
