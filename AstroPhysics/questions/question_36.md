### Question 36
**6] India has recently landed its Moon Impact Probe on the Moon. among the following countries, which one landed such probe on the Moon earlier? [2009]**


a) Australia 
b) Canada

c) China 
d) Japan

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) China

**Explanation:**
- **Soviet Union (now Russi
a)**

- The Soviet Union was the first to achieve a successful unmanned landing on the Moon with Luna 2 in 1959, and later Luna 9 achieved the first soft landing on February 3, 1966.

- **United States**

- The United States, through NASA, was the first country to land a manned mission on the Moon. The United States achieved the first manned Moon landing on July 20, 1969, with Apollo 11. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first humans to walk on the lunar surface.

- **China**

- China initiated its lunar exploration program, named after the Chinese moon goddess Chang’e, with the launch of Chang’e 1 in 2007. Chang’e 3 (2013) and Chang’e 4 (2019) achieved soft landings on the Moon, with Chang’e 4 landing on the far side.

- **The first two Chinese moon missions i.e. Chang’e 1 and Chang’e 2, only consisted of orbiters. The first Chinese landing on moon came in 2013 with Chang’e 3.**

- **India**

- India’s Chandrayaan missions are a series of lunar exploration missions undertaken by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). There have been three missions so far: Chandrayaan-1, Chandrayaan-2, and Chandrayaan-3.

- **Chandrayaan-1 (2008):** India’s first lunar probe, launched in October 2008. It consisted of an orbiter and an impactor. The orbiter carried out a comprehensive study of the Moon’s surface, while the impactor provided data on the lunar soil composition. Chandrayaan-1 is credited with making the historic discovery of water on the Moon.

- **Chandrayaan-2 (2019):** India’s second lunar mission, launched in July 2019. It consisted of an orbiter, a lander (Vikram), and a rover (Pragyan). Unfortunately, the Vikram lander crash-landed on the lunar surface during its descent. However, the orbiter continues to operate successfully, studying the Moon’s surface and environment.

- **Chandrayaan-3 (2023):** Launched in July 2023, Chandrayaan-3 is a follow-up mission to Chandrayaan-2 designed to demonstrate India’s capability for a soft landing on the Moon. The mission consists of a lander and a rover (Pragyan). Unlike Chandrayaan-2, Chandrayaan-3 successfully landed its lander Vikram on the lunar surface in August 2023, making it the first Indian spacecraft to do so. The Pragyan rover is currently deployed on the lunar surface and carrying out scientific studies.

- **It appears that China did not do any landing on moon before 2013. The question was asked in 2009 and hence, none of the option given is correct.**
