### Question 46
**16] Assertion (
A): To orbit around the Sun, the planet Mars takes lesser time than the time taken by the Earth.: [2006]**

Reason (R): The diameter of the Planet Mars is less than that of the Earth.

a) Both A and R are individually true and R is the correct explanation of A.

b) Both A and R are individually tru but R is not the correct explanation of A.

c) A is True but R is false

d) A is false but R is true

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) A is false but R is true

**Explanation:**
- Mars is farther from the Sun than Earth, so its orbital path is larger. Mars takes approximately 687 Earth days (or about 1.88 Earth years) to complete one orbit around the Sun. In comparison, Earth takes approximately 365.25 days (or about 1 Earth year) to complete one orbit around the Sun. **Therefore, Earth completes its orbit around the Sun in a shorter time period than Mars i.e. Assertion is false.**

- The diameter of Earth is approximately 12,742 kilometers (7,918 miles), whereas, the diameter of Mars is approximately 6,779 kilometers (4,212 miles). **Thus, Reason is true.**
