### Question 48
**18]** **Consider the following statements in respect of India’s advanced satellite INSAT 4A: [2006]**

1. INSAT . 4A was launched in December, 2005 from New Mexico.
2. The European commercial launch service provider Arianespace was associated with the launch of INSAT . 4A.
3. Tata-Sky a digital cable service provider is associated with DTH television broadcasting services from INSAT-4A

Which of the statements given above are correct?

a) 1, 2 and 3 
b) 1 and 2, only

c) 2 and 3, only 
d) 1 and 3, only

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 2 and 3, only

**Explanation:**
- INSAT-4A is an Indian communication satellite, part of the Indian National Satellite System (INSAT).

- It was launched in Dec 2005 from the Guiana Space Centre in Kourou, French Guiana.

- The European commercial launch service provider Arianespace was responsible for the launch of INSAT-4A using their Ariane-5 rocket.

- The applications of INSAT-4A included Direct-to-Home (DTH) television services, Very Small Aperture Terminal (VSAT) services and Telecommunication and data transmission.

- And yes, Tata Sky, a digital cable service provider, utilizes the Ku-band transponders on INSAT-4A for its Direct-to-Home (DTH) television broadcasting services.

- **Thus, 2nd and 3rd statements are true, while the first one isn’t.**
