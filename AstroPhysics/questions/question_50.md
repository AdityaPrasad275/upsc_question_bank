### Question 50
**20] INSAT-3E, India’s communication satellite, was launched in 2003 from: [2004]**


a) French Guyana 
b) Seychelles

c) Mauritius 
d) Mauritania

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) French Guyana

**Explanation:**
- INSAT-3E, India’s communication satellite, was launched on September 28, 2003, from the Guiana Space Centre in Kourou, French Guiana.
