### Question 58
**28] Who amongst the following was the Chairman of ISRO when INSAT3B was launched? [2001]**


a) Anil Kakodkar 
b) Abdul Kalam

c) K. Kasturirangan 
d) U. R. Rao

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) K. Kasturirangan

**Explanation:**
- INSAT-3B, an Indian communication satellite, was launched on March 22, 2000.

- Dr. K. Kasturirangan served as the Chairman of ISRO from 1994 to 2003.
