### Question 60
**30] Who is the scientist in whose honour the “Chandra” X-ray telescope has been named? [2001]**


a) Chandrasekhar Venkat Raman

b) Jagdish Chandra Bose

c) Prafulla Chandra Roy

d) Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar

**Explanation:**
- The Chandra X-ray Observatory was named in honour of the late Nobel laureate Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar.

- Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar was an Indian-American astrophysicist who made significant contributions to our understanding of stellar structure and evolution.

- His most famous work includes the Chandrasekhar limit, which describes the maximum mass of a stable white dwarf star, beyond which it will collapse into a neutron star or black hole.
