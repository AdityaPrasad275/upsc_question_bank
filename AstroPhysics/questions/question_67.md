### Question 67
**37] Which one of the following stars is nearest to the earth? [1997]**


a) Polaris 
b) Alpha Centauri

c) Sun 
d) Sirius

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Sun

**Explanation:**
- Sun: The Sun is the closest star to Earth, located approximately 150 million kilometers away. It is the center of our solar system.

- Alpha Centauri: Alpha Centauri is a binary star system located about 4.37 light-years away from Earth, making it the closest star system to our solar system.

- Sirius: Sirius, also known as the Dog Star, is one of the brightest stars in the sky and is located about 8.6 light-years away from Earth.

- Polaris: Polaris, also known as the North Star, is located at a distance of about 434 light-years away from Earth.
