### Question 70
**40] A meteor is: [1995]**


a) A rapidly moving star

b) A piece of matter which has entered the earth’s atmosphere from outer spac

c) Part of a constellation

d) A comet without a tail

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) A piece of matter which has entered the earth’s atmosphere from outer spac

**Explanation:**
- A meteoroid is a small rock or particle from space, typically originating from a comet or asteroid.

- A meteor is a streak of light in the sky caused by a meteoroid entering the Earth’s atmosphere and burning up due to friction with the air.

- When a meteoroid enters the Earth’s atmosphere and vaporizes, it creates a bright streak of light called a meteor. This is often referred to as a “shooting star” or “falling star.”

- When the Earth passes through the debris field left by a comet, multiple meteors can be seen, creating a meteor shower.

- Thus, 
b) is the most appropriate answer.
