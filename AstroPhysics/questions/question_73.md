### Question 73
**43] Cryogenic engines find applications in: [1995]**


a) Sub-marine propulsion

b) Frost-free refrigerators

c) Rocket technology

d) Researches in superconductivity

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Rocket technology

**Explanation:**
- The term “cryogenic” comes from the Greek words “kryos,” meaning cold or freezing, and “genos,” meaning born or produced. Therefore, cryogenic pertains to the production and behavior of materials at very low temperatures.

- Cryogenic engines are a type of rocket engine that use cryogenic propellants, which are fuels and oxidizers that are stored at extremely low temperatures. These engines are commonly used in space exploration due to their high efficiency and performance.

- Hence option 
c) is correct.
