### Question 9
**1] Which one of the following is a reason why astronomical distances are measured in light-years? [2021]
**

a) Distances among stellar bodies do not change

b) Gravity of stellar bodies does not change

c) Light always travels in straight line

d) Speed of light is always same

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Speed of light is always same

**Explanation:**
- Astronomical distances are measured in light-years because the universe is so vast that using conventional units like kilometres or miles becomes impractical.

- A light-year, which is the distance light travels in one year (about 9.46 trillion kilometres), provides a more manageable and comprehensible way to express these enormous distances.

- This unit leverages the **constant speed of light, making it a clear and consistent standard** for astronomers to describe the vast separations between celestial objects.

- **Hence, d is the correct answer.**
