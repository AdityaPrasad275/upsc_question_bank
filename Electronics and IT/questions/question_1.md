### Question
**1] ****In the context of electric vehicle batteries, consider the following elements:** **[2025/A-43]

**I. Cobalt
II. Graphite
III. Lithium
IV. Nickel
How many of the above usually make up battery cathodes?

a) Only one
b) Only two
c) Only three
d) All the four

**Answer:**
Ans: c) Only three

**Explanation:**
- **Statement I: Correct** – Cobalt is commonly used in battery cathodes to enhance stability and energy density.



- **Statement II: Incorrect** – Graphite is not part of the cathode; it is typically used in the anode of lithium-ion batteries.



- **Statement III: Correct** – Lithium forms the key active component of the cathode in most electric vehicle batteries.



- **Statement IV: Correct** – Nickel is also used in cathodes to increase capacity and reduce cobalt dependence.



- Hence, correct Ans: c) ) Only three
