### Question
**4] With reference to “Blockchain Technology”, consider the following statements: [2020]**

1. It is a public ledger that everyone can inspect, but which no single user controls.
2. The structure and design of blockchain is such that all the data in it are about cryptocurrency only.
3. Applications that depend on basic features of blockchain can be developed without anybody’s permission.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
a) 1 only
b) 1 and 2 only
c) 2 only
d) 1 and 3 only

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- **Statement 1 is true.**



- Blockchain is indeed a public ledger, meaning that transactions recorded on the blockchain are visible to everyone. However, no single user or entity controls the entire blockchain. Instead, it operates on a decentralized network where multiple participants (nodes) maintain and validate the transactions.



- **Statement 2 is false.**



- While blockchain technology was originally developed for cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, its potential applications extend far beyond just financial transactions. Blockchain can be used to record and verify any type of digital transaction or data, including contracts, supply chain information, voting records, and more. Cryptocurrencies are just one of many possible applications of blockchain technology.



- **Statement 3 is true.**



- Blockchain technology allows for the development of decentralized applications (DApps) that operate without central authority or control. These applications leverage the basic features of blockchain, such as immutability, transparency, and decentralization, to provide various services without requiring permission from any central entity. However, it’s important to note that while the development of DApps may not require permission, their deployment and integration with existing systems may be subject to regulatory requirements.
