### Question
**4] In the context of wearable technology, which of the following tasks is/are accomplished by wearable devices? [2019]**

1. Location identification of a person
2. Sleep monitoring of a person
3. Assisting the hearing impaired person

Select the correct answer using the code given below:
a) 1 only
b) 2 and 3 only
c) 3 only
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Wearable technology, often referred to as wearables, refers to electronic devices that are worn on the body or integrated into clothing and accessories. The examples include smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, wearable medical devices etc.



- These devices can have GPS embedded in them which will help in location identification. These also have various sensor which can monitor the sleep and other activity of a person. These devices can also act as a hearing aid and assist the hearing-impaired person.



- **Thus, all the statements are true.**
