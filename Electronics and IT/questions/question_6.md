### Question
**2] Which one of the following words/phrases is most appropriately used to denote “an interoperable network of 3D virtual worlds that can be accessed simultaneously by millions of users, who can exert property rights over virtual items”? [2024]

**a) Big data analytics
b) Cryptography
c) Metaverse
d) Virtual matrix

**Answer:**
Ans: c) Metaverse

**Explanation:**
- Big data analytics is the process of analyzing vast datasets to uncover hidden patterns, correlations, and trends. For example, e-commerce platforms analyze customer browsing and purchasing data to recommend products and optimize inventory management.



- Cryptography is the science of securing communication and data through encryption techniques. By transforming readable information into an unreadable format, cryptography ensures that only authorized individuals can access the original data.



- **The metaverse refers to a digital universe where users can interact with each other and the environment through immersive technologies like virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).** In metaverse, Users can own, trade, and exert control over digital assets such as virtual land, clothing for avatars, and digital goods. Blockchain and NFTs (non-fungible tokens) play a key role in ensuring verifiable ownership and transferability of these virtual assets.



- A virtual matrix refers to an interconnected digital system where different elements or subsystems are integrated to function cohesively. It enables the central control and coordination of distributed components.



- Thus, c) Metaverse is the correct option.
