### Question
**1] With reference to the sectors of the Indian economy, consider the following pairs [2024]

**Economic activity – Sector
1. Storage of agricultural produce – Secondary
2. Dairy farm – Primary
3. Mineral exploration – Tertiary
4. Weaving cloth – Secondary

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched? 
a) Only one
b) Only two
c) Only three
d) All four

**Answer:**
Ans: b) Only two

**Explanation:**
- Primary sector involves the extraction and harvesting of natural resources. This sector is focused on raw materials that are directly taken from the earth e.g. growing crops (like rice or wheat), mining coal, extracting oil, fishing, logging etc.



- Secondary sector involves processing raw materials from the primary sector into finished goods or products. It is focused on manufacturing and construction e.g. producing cars, construction of buildings, refining oil into petrol, making textiles or clothes etc.



- Tertiary sector is focused on providing services rather than goods. It supports the functioning of the primary and secondary sectors by offering various services e.g. teachers, doctors, bank employees, software developers, taxi drivers, retail store workers etc.



- 1] Storage of agricultural produce falls under the tertiary sector since it is a service related to the handling and distribution of agricultural goods rather than manufacturing. **This makes first pair incorrect.**



- 2] A dairy farm is part of the primary sector because it involves the production of raw agricultural products (milk) directly from animals. **Thus, second pair is correct**.



- 3] Mineral exploration is about finding and evaluating mineral deposits. Although it may appear service sector, according to National Industrial Classification 2008, Mining exploration pertains to primary sector. **Thus, third pair is incorrect.**



- Weaving cloth is a manufacturing process, placing it in the secondary sector as it transforms raw materials (like cotton or silk) into finished goods, **making fourth pair, correct.**



- Thus, b) Only two, is the correct answer.
