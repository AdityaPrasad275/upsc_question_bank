### Question
**1] With reference to India economy, consider the following statements: [2015]**

1. The rate of growth of real Gross Domestic Product has steadily increased in the last decade.
2. The Gross Domestic Product at market prices (in rupees) has steadily increased in the last decade
Which of the statement given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only
b) 2 only
c) Both 1 and 2
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: b) 2 only

**Explanation:**
The rate of growth of real Gross Domestic Product has steadily increased in the last decade.

- This statement suggests that the growth rate of real GDP has consistently risen over the last decade. However, this may not be entirely accurate. While India has experienced overall economic growth over the past decade, the growth rate has not been steadily increasing every single year. There have been fluctuations and periods of slower growth as well. Therefore, this statement is not entirely correct.





The Gross Domestic Product at market prices (in rupees) has steadily increased in the last decade.

- This statement indicates that the nominal GDP in rupees has steadily increased over the last decade. Again, while there has been overall economic growth, there have been fluctuations in GDP growth rates over the past decade. Therefore, this statement is also not entirely correct.
