### Question
**3] A decrease in tax to GDP ratio of a country indicates which of the following? [2015]**

1. Slowing economic growth rates
2. Less equitable distribution of national income
Select the correct answer using the code given below.

a) 1 only
b) 2 only
c) Both 1 and 2
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: a) 1 only

**Explanation:**
- Slowing economic growth rates: A decrease in the tax-to-GDP ratio could indicate slowing economic growth rates. If the economy is growing at a slower pace, tax revenues may not grow as quickly as the overall economy, leading to a decrease in the tax-to-GDP ratio. Therefore, option 1 is plausible.



- Less equitable distribution of national income: A decrease in the tax-to-GDP ratio might also imply a less equitable distribution of national income. If the tax burden is reduced, it could potentially benefit higher-income individuals more than lower-income individuals, exacerbating income inequality. However, this implication is not directly evident from the decrease in the tax-to-GDP ratio alone.
