### Question
**1] Under which of the following circumstances may ‘capital gains’ arise? [2012]**

1. When there is an increase in the sales of a product
2. When there is a natural increase in the value of the property owned
3. When you purchase a painting and there is a growth in its value due to increase in its popularity

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:
a) 1 Only&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) 2 &amp; 3 Only
c) 2 Only&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) 1,2 &amp; 3 Only

**Answer:**
Ans:  b) 2 &amp; 3 Only

**Explanation:**
Capital gains arise when you sell an asset for a higher price than you bought it for. Here’s why the given options are correct or incorrect:






- **Increase in sales of a product:**&nbsp;This is related to the revenue of a business, not capital gains. Capital gains specifically refer to the profit made from selling a capital asset, not the income generated by selling products or services.



- **Natural increase in the value of property owned:**&nbsp;This is a scenario where capital gains can arise. If you buy a property and its value increases over time due to natural market trends or development in the area, you can make a capital gain when you sell it.



- **Growth in value of a painting due to popularity:**&nbsp;This is another situation where capital gains can occur. If you buy a painting and its value appreciates due to increased popularity of the artist or the genre, you can earn a capital gain by selling it at a higher price.




Therefore, only options 2 and 3 reprepresent circumstances where capital gains can arise.
