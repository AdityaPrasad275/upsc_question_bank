### Question
**9] Global capital flows to developing countries increased significantly during the nineties. In view of the East Asian financial crisis and Latin American experience, which type of inflow is good for the host country? [2002]**

a) Commercial loans
b) Foreign Direct Investment
c) Foreign Portfolio Investment
d) External Commercial Borrowings

**Answer:**
Ans:  b) Foreign Direct Investment

**Explanation:**
**Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), on the other hand, offers several advantages:**






- **Long-term investment:**&nbsp;FDI typically involves establishing a physical presence in the host country. This encourages a longer-term commitment from investors, bringing stability compared to short-term financial investments.



- **Transfer of technology and skills:**&nbsp;FDI can lead to the transfer of technology, skills, and knowledge from foreign firms to the host country. This can contribute to productivity growth and economic development.



- **Job creation:**&nbsp;FDI often creates jobs in the host country, boosting economic activity and local incomes.



- **Infrastructure development:**&nbsp;FDI can play a role in developing infrastructure, which is essential for long-term economic growth.
