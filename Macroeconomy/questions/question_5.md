### Question
**1] Which of the following activities constitute a real sector in the economy? [2022]**

1. Farmers harvesting their crops
2. Textile mills converting raw cotton into fabrics
3. A commercial bank lending money to a trading company
4. A corporate body issuing Rupee Denominated Bonds overseas
Select the correct answer using the codes given below

a) 1 and 2
b) 2, 3 and 4 only
c) 1, 3 and 4 only
d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: a) 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- Farmers harvesting their crops – This activity involves the production of agricultural goods, which is part of the real sector.



- Textile mills converting raw cotton into fabrics – This activity involves the manufacturing of goods (fabrics) from raw materials (cotton), which is also part of the real sector.



- A commercial bank lending money to a trading company – This activity involves financial intermediation and lending, which is part of the financial sector, not the real sector.



- A corporate body issuing Rupee Denominated Bonds overseas – This activity involves financial instruments and capital markets, which are also part of the financial sector, not the real sector.




Based on the analysis:





a) 1 and 2 – These activities involve real sector activities. 
b) 2, 3, and 4 only – Options 3 and 4 involve financial sector activities. 
c) 1, 3, and 4 only – Option 2 involves a real sector activity. 
d) 1, 2, 3, and 4 – Options 3 and 4 involve financial sector activities.
