### Question
**1] Which among the following steps is most likely to be taken at the time of an economic recession? [2021]

**a) Cut in tax rates accompanied by increase in interest rate
b) Increase in expenditure on public projects
c) Increase in tax rates accompanied by reduction of interest rate
d) Reduction of expenditure on public projects

**Answer:**
Ans: b) Increase in expenditure on public projects

**Explanation:**
- **Recessions**&nbsp;are characterized by a decline in economic activity, leading to decreased employment and consumer spending.



- **Increased expenditure on public projects**&nbsp;injects money into the economy by creating jobs in construction and related industries. This increased spending can stimulate economic growth.



- **Cutting tax rates**&nbsp;might put more money in people’s pockets, but during a recession, people are more likely to save than spend.



- **Raising interest rates**&nbsp;is a tool used to combat inflation, which is not the primary concern during a recession. In fact, recessions are often accompanied by deflation (falling prices).



- **Reducing expenditure on public projects**&nbsp;would further decrease economic activity, worsening the recession.




Therefore, increasing government spending on public projects is the most likely action taken to counteract a recession.
