### Question
**2] Consider the following statements: [2021]**

Other things remaining unchanged, market demand for a good might increase if
1) Price of its substitute increases
2) Price of its complement increases
3) The good is an inferior good and income of the consumers increases
4) Its price falls
Which of the above statements are correct?

a) 1 and 4 only
b) 2, 3 and 4
c) 1, 3 and 4
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: a) 1 and 4 only

**Explanation:**
- Price of its substitute increases – When the price of a substitute for a good increases, consumers may choose to buy the original good instead, leading to an increase in demand for the original good. So, this statement is correct.



- Price of its complement increases – When the price of a complement for a good increases, consumers may buy less of both goods, leading to a decrease in demand for the original good. Therefore, this statement is incorrect.



- The good is an inferior good and income of the consumers increases – Inferior goods are those for which demand decreases as consumer incomes rise. Therefore, if the good is inferior and consumer incomes increase, the demand for this good would decrease. This statement is incorrect.



- Its price falls – According to the law of demand, when the price of a good falls, its quantity demanded increases, assuming other factors remain constant. So, this statement is correct.
