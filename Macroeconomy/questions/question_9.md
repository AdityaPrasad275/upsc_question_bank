### Question
**1] Consider the following statements: [2019]**

1. Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) exchange rates are calculated by comparing the prices of the same basket of goods and services in different countries.
2. In terms of PPP dollars, India is the sixth largest economy in the world.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only
b) 2 only
c) Both 1 and 2
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: a) 1 only

**Explanation:**
Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) exchange rates are calculated by comparing the prices of the same basket of goods and services in different countries.

- This statement is generally true. PPP exchange rates are indeed calculated based on comparing the prices of a basket of goods and services in different countries to determine the relative value of currencies.





In terms of PPP dollars, India is the sixth largest economy in the world.

- This statement is not necessarily correct. While India has been ranked as one of the largest economies in the world when measured by GDP at PPP, the specific ranking might vary depending on the year and the source of data. Therefore, it’s not certain that India is the sixth largest economy in the world in terms of PPP dollars.






Given this reassessment, statement 1 is correct, but statement 2 is not necessarily correct.
