### Question
**1] Which one of the following statements correctly describes the meaning of legal tender money? [2018]

**a) The money which is tendered in courts of law to defray the fee of legal cases
b) The money which a creditor is under compulsion to accept in settlement of his claims
c) The bank money in the form of cheques, drafts, bill of exchange, etc.
d) The metallic money in circulation in a country

**Answer:**
Ans: b) The money which a creditor is under compulsion to accept in settlement of his claims

**Explanation:**
- **Legal tender**&nbsp;refers to any form of money that is recognized by law as a means to settle a debt or financial obligation.



- In simpler terms, if you owe someone money, they are legally obligated to accept&nbsp;**legal tender**&nbsp;to pay off your debt. This applies to public and private debts, including taxes, contracts, and legal fines.




Let’s analyze the other options:






- **a) The money which is tendered in courts of law to defray the fee of legal cases:**&nbsp;While courts might accept legal tender for fees, this doesn’t define legal tender itself. Legal tender applies to a broader range of debts, not just court fees.



- **c) The bank money in the form of cheques, drafts, bill of exchange, etc.:**&nbsp;These are negotiable instruments, not legal tender. They represent a promise to pay but are not considered legal tender on their own. They need to be cleared or converted to cash (legal tender) to settle a debt.



- **d) The metallic money in circulation in a country:**&nbsp;Legal tender can include physical currency (coins and bills) as well as other forms designated by law. It’s not restricted to just metallic money.
