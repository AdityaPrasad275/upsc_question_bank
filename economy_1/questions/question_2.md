### Question
**2] With reference to physical capital in Indian economy, consider the following pairs: [2024]
**Items – Category 
Farmer’s plough – Working capital
Computer – Fixed capital
Yarn used by the weaver – Fixed capital
Petrol – Working capital

How many of the above pairs are matched?
a) Only one
b) Only two
c) Only three
d) All four

**Answer:**
Ans: b) Only two

**Explanation:**
- Fixed capital refers to long-term assets that a company uses in its operations to produce goods and services. These assets are not expected to be converted into cash within a year.



- Working capital refers to the short-term assets that a company needs to manage its day-to-day operations. It represents the liquidity available to a business for its operational needs.



- According to above definition, farmer’s plough, computer are fixed capital while other two i.e. yarn and petrol are working capital.



- Thus, b) only two is correct answer.
