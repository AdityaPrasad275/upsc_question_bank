### Question
**2] Economic growth is usually coupled with? [2011]**

a) Deflation&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) Inflation.
c) Stagflation&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) Hyperinflation

**Answer:**
Ans:  b) Inflation.

**Explanation:**
Economic growth is typically coupled with inflation.





Inflation refers to the general increase in prices of goods and services over time, resulting in a decrease in the purchasing power of money. When an economy experiences economic growth, there is often increased demand for goods and services, leading to upward pressure on prices. This increase in demand, combined with factors such as expansionary monetary policies and rising production costs, can contribute to inflationary pressures.
