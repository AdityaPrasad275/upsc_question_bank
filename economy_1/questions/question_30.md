### Question
**7] Which one among the following countries has the lowest GDP per capita? [2003]**

a) China&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;b) India
c) Indonesia&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) Sri Lanka

**Answer:**
Ans:  b) India

**Explanation:**
a) China – China has experienced significant economic growth over the years, and its GDP per capita has risen steadily. It is no longer among the countries with the lowest GDP per capita.





b) India – While India’s GDP per capita has been increasing, it remains relatively low compared to many other countries.





c) Indonesia – Indonesia’s GDP per capita is higher than India’s but still lower than many developed countries.





d) Sri Lanka – Sri Lanka’s GDP per capita is generally lower than the other countries listed.





Given this analysis, the country with the lowest GDP per capita among the options provided is:





b) India
