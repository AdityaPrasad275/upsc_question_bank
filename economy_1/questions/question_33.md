### Question
**10] A country is said to be in debt trap if: [2002]**

a) It has to abide by the conditionalities imposed by the International Monetary Fund
b) It has to borrow to make interest payments to on outstanding loans
c) It has been refused loans or aid by creditors abroad
d) The World Bank charges a very high rate of interest on outstanding as well as new loans

**Answer:**
Ans: b) It has to borrow to make interest payments to on outstanding loans

**Explanation:**
- **Debt Trap:** A debt trap is a situation where a country accumulates so much debt that it becomes difficult or impossible to repay it. The burden of servicing the debt (paying interest) stifles economic growth and limits the country’s ability to invest in development.



- **Making Interest Payments:** This option highlights a crucial aspect of a debt trap. If a country has to continually borrow money just to make interest payments on existing loans, it’s a sign of financial distress and potential insolvency. The borrowed funds aren’t going towards productive investments but are used to service existing debt, hindering economic progress.
