### Question
**12] Consider the following States: [2001]**

I. Gujarat
II. Karnataka
III. Maharashtra
IV. Tamil Nadu.

The descending order of these States with reference to their level of Per Capita Net State Domestic Product is
a) I, III, IV, II&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) II, I, IV, III
c) I, III, II, IV&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) III, I, II, IV

**Answer:**
Ans: d) III, I, II, IV

**Explanation:**
The correct answer is option 4. This means that, based on the Per Capita Net State Domestic Product, Maharashtra has the highest, followed by Gujarat, then Karnataka and finally, Tamil Nadu. So, this shows the economic performance of each state in relation to their population.





Per Capita Net State Domestic Product is a measure of the average economic output per person in a specific region. It is calculated by taking the total economic output (Net State Domestic Product) and dividing it by the total population.
