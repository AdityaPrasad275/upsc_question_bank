### Question
**15] The new Gross Domestic Product (GDP) series released by the Central Statistical Organisation (CSO) in February 1999 is with reference to base price of: [2000]**

a) 1991-’92&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) 1992-’93
c) 1993′-94&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) 1994′-95

**Answer:**
Ans:  c) 1993′-94

**Explanation:**
The question specifies that the new GDP series released in February 1999 uses a base price of a specific year. This base year serves as a reference point for measuring economic growth and allows for comparisons of GDP figures over time by adjusting for inflation.





By analyzing the answer choices and considering the timing of the series release (February 1999), we can eliminate options:






- **a) 1991-’92:**&nbsp;This base year would be too early for a series released in 1999.



- **b) 1992-’93:**&nbsp;Similar to option (a), this base year predates the series release.



- **d) 1994′-95:**&nbsp;While closer to 1999, it’s less likely as a new series wouldn’t typically use a base year just a few years prior to its release.
