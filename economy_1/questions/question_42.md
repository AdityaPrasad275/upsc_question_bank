### Question
**18] Since 1980, the share of the tertiary sector in the total GDP of India has: [1999]**

a) Shown an increasing trend
b) Shown a decreasing trend
c) Remained constant
d) Been fluctuating

**Answer:**
Ans:  a) Shown an increasing trend

**Explanation:**
- **Tertiary Sector:**&nbsp;This sector encompasses services like trade, transportation, communication, finance, healthcare, education, and tourism.




Since the 1980s, the Indian economy has undergone a significant structural transformation. Here’s the trend:






- **Shift from Primary to Tertiary:**&nbsp;The share of the primary sector (agriculture, forestry, fishing) in India’s GDP has been declining, while the share of the tertiary sector has been&nbsp;**increasing**.



- **Secondary Sector (Manufacturing):**&nbsp;The share of the secondary sector (manufacturing, construction, utilities) has also seen some growth but likely not as significant as the tertiary sector.
