### Question
**19] A consumer is said to be in equilibrium, if: [1998]**

a) He is able to fulfil his need with a given level of income
b) He is able to live in full comforts with a given level of income
c) He can fulfil his needs without consumption of certain items
d) He is able to locate new sources of income

**Answer:**
Ans:  a) He is able to fulfil his need with a given level of income

**Explanation:**
A consumer is said to be in equilibrium when: a) He is able to fulfill his need with a given level of income.





Consumer equilibrium occurs when the consumer allocates their income in such a way that they maximize their utility, given their budget constraint. In other words, the consumer is satisfied with the combination of goods and services they are able to purchase with their income, and they have no incentive to reallocate their spending to increase their satisfaction further.





Therefore, option a) is the correct statement describing consumer equilibrium.
