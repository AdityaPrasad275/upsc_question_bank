### Question
**23] Which one of the following is correct regarding stabilization and structural adjustment as two components of the new economic policy adopted in India? [1996]**

a) Stabilization is a gradual, multi-step process while structural adjustment is a quick adaptation process
b) Structural adjustment is a gradual, multi-step process, while stabilization is a quick adaptation process
c) Stabilization and structural adjustment are very similar and complementary policies. It is difficult to separate one
from the other
d) Stabilization mainly deals with a set of policies which are to be implemented by the Central Government while structural adjustment is to be set in motion by the State Governments

**Answer:**
Ans:  b) Structural adjustment is a gradual, multi-step process, while stabilization is a quick adaptation process

**Explanation:**
a) Stabilization is a gradual, multi-step process while structural adjustment is a quick adaptation process – This option mischaracterizes the nature of stabilization and structural adjustment. Stabilization typically involves short-term measures aimed at correcting imbalances in the economy, such as reducing inflation or controlling budget deficits, while structural adjustment involves longer-term reforms aimed at addressing underlying structural issues in the economy, such as deregulation, privatization, and trade liberalization.





b) Structural adjustment is a gradual, multi-step process, while stabilization is a quick adaptation process – This option accurately describes the distinction between stabilization and structural adjustment. Stabilization measures are often implemented more quickly to address immediate economic challenges, while structural adjustment measures are implemented gradually over time to promote sustainable economic growth.





c) Stabilization and structural adjustment are very similar and complementary policies. It is difficult to separate one from the other – This option is incorrect. While stabilization and structural adjustment may complement each other in some cases, they are distinct policy approaches with different objectives and timeframes.





d) Stabilization mainly deals with a set of policies which are to be implemented by the Central Government while structural adjustment is to be set in motion by the State Governments – This option incorrectly attributes the responsibility for stabilization and structural adjustment to different levels of government. Both stabilization and structural adjustment policies are typically implemented at the national level, often under the direction of the central government.
