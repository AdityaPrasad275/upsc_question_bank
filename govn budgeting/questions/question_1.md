**1] ****Consider the following statements:** **[2025/A-10]

**I. Capital receipts create a liability or cause a reduction in the assets of the Government.
II. Borrowings and disinvestment are capital receipts.
III. Interest received on loans creates a liability of the Government.
Which of the statements given above are correct?


a) I and II only

b) II and III only

c) I and III only

d) I, II and III

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) I and II only

**Explanation:**
- **Statement 1: Correct** – Capital receipts either create a liability (like borrowings) or reduce assets (like disinvestment or recovery of loans).

- **Statement 2: Correct** – Borrowings create a liability, and disinvestment reduces government assets. Hence both are classified as capital receipts.

- **Statement 3: Incorrect** – Interest received on loans is a revenue receipt, not a capital one. It does not create any liability for the Government; instead, it is income.

- Correct Ans: 
a) ) I and II only
