### Question 14
**1] With reference to Union Budget, which of the following is/are covered under Non-Plan Expenditure? [2014]**

1. Defence expenditure
2. Interest payments
3. Salaries and pensions
4. Subsidies
Select the correct answer using the code given below.


a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1, 2, 3 and 4

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
- **Defence expenditure**: Defence expenditure is a part of Non-Plan Expenditure. It falls under the category of expenditure that is not directly related to planned development activities.

- **Interest payments**: Interest payments on loans and borrowings are a significant component of Non-Plan Expenditure. These payments are made irrespective of development plans.

- **Salaries and pensions**: Salaries and pensions of government employees are included in Non-Plan Expenditure as they are recurring expenditures unrelated to planned development activities.

- **Subsidies**: Subsidies are typically part of Non-Plan Expenditure. They are provided to support various sectors or segments of the economy and are not directly related to planned development activities.

Considering this, all the options listed (1, 2, 3, and 4) are covered under Non-Plan Expenditure.
