### Question 18
**2] In the union budget 2011-12, a full exemption from the basic customs duty was extended to the bio-based asphalt (biosphalt). What is the importance of this material? [2011]**

1. Unlike traditional asphalt, bio-asphalt is not based on fossil fuels.
2. Biosphalt can be made from non-renewable resources.
3. Bioasphalt can be made from organic waste materials.
4. It is eco-friendly to use bioasphalt for surfacing of the roads.

Which the correct answer using the codes given below?

a) 1,2,and 3 only 
b) 1,3 and 4 only.

c) 2 and 4 only 
d) 1,2,3, and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 1,3 and 4 only.

**Explanation:**
- **Fossil Fuel Dependence (Correct):** Bio-asphalt is an alternative to traditional asphalt, which is derived from petroleum products. This exemption encourages the use of bio-asphalt, reducing reliance on fossil fuels.

- **Non-renewable Resources (Incorrect):** While some bio-asphalt feedstock might come from sources that aren’t strictly renewable (e.g., used oils), the concept is to utilize organic materials that can be replenished.

- **Organic Waste Materials (Correct):** Bio-asphalt can be made from various organic waste materials like vegetable oils, recycled plastic, or even agricultural waste. This promotes waste management and resource recovery.

- **Eco-friendly (Correct):** Bio-asphalt production and use can be more environmentally friendly compared to traditional asphalt. It can potentially reduce greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on non-renewable resources.
