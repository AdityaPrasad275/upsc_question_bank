### Question 2
**2] ****Suppose the revenue expenditure is ₹80,000 crores and the revenue receipts of the Government are ₹60,000 crores. The Government budget also shows borrowings of ₹10,000 crores and interest payments of ₹6,000 crores. Which of the following statements are correct?** **[2025/A-61]

**I. Revenue deficit is ₹20,000 crores.
II. Fiscal deficit is ₹10,000 crores.
III. Primary deficit is ₹4,000 crores.
Select the correct answer using the code given below.


a) I and II only

b) II and III only

c) I and III only

d) I, II and III

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) I, II and III

**Explanation:**
- **Statement I: Correct** – Revenue deficit = Revenue expenditure – Revenue receipts = 80,000 – 60,000 = ₹20,000 crores.

- **Statement II: Correct** – Fiscal deficit = Total expenditure – Total receipts (excluding borrowings). Since the government borrows ₹10,000 crores to meet the gap, fiscal deficit = ₹10,000 crores.

- **Statement III: Correct** – Primary deficit = Fiscal deficit – Interest payments = 10,000 – 6,000 = ₹4,000 crores.

- Correct Ans: 
d) ) I, II and III
