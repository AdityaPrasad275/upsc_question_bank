### Question 20
**4] The authorization for the withdrawal of funds from the consolidated fund of India must come from? [2011]**


a) The president of India.

b) The parliament of India.

c) The prime minister of India.

d) The union finance minister.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) The parliament of India.

**Explanation:**
- The Consolidated Fund of India is the central reservoir of all the receipts of the Government of India.

- The Constitution of India mandates that no money can be withdrawn from this fund without the approval of Parliament.

- This ensures parliamentary control over public spending and promotes fiscal responsibility.

The other options are not responsible for authorizing withdrawals from the Consolidated Fund:

- **President of India:** While the President plays an important role in the Indian government, they don’t have direct control over authorizing such withdrawals.

- **Prime Minister of India:** The Prime Minister leads the government but doesn’t hold sole authority over approving spending from the Consolidated Fund.

- **Union Finance Minister:** The Finance Minister is a key figure in presenting the budget and managing finances, but parliamentary approval is still required for withdrawals.
