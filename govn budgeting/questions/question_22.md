### Question 22
**6] When the annual budget is not passed by the lok sabha? [2011]**


a) The budget is modified and presented again.

b) The budget is referred to the rajya sabha for suggestions.

c) The union finance minister is asked to resign.

d) The prime minister submits the resignation of council of ministers.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) The prime minister submits the resignation of council of ministers.

**Explanation:**
- The Lok Sabha is the lower house of Parliament and plays a crucial role in approving the budget. Its rejection signifies a lack of confidence in the government’s financial plans.

- By convention, the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers (cabinet) are responsible for presenting and getting the budget passed. Failure to do so can be seen as a loss of confidence in their leadership.

The other options are not the most common consequences:

- **(
a) Modified and Presented Again:** This might happen in rare situations, but typically only after addressing concerns raised during the initial presentation.

- **(
b) Referred to Rajya Sabha:** The Rajya Sabha (upper house) has limited powers regarding the budget. It can’t directly pass or reject the budget, but can offer recommendations.

- **(
c) Finance Minister Resignation:** While the Finance Minister might face some pressure, traditionally, the entire Council of Ministers takes responsibility for budget passage.
