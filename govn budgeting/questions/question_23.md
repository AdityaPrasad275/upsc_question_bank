### Question 23
**1] With reference to the National Investment Fund to which the disinvestment proceeds are routed, consider the following statements: [2010]**

1. The assets in the National Investment Fund are managed by the Union Ministry of Finance.
2. The National Investment Fund is to be maintained within the Consolidated Fund of India.
3. Certain Asset Management Companies are appointed as the fund managers.
4. A certain proportion of annual income is used for financing select social sectors.

Which of the statement given above is/are correct?

a) 1 and 2 
b) 2 only

c) 3 and 4 
d) 3 only

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
- **Managed by Finance Ministry (Incorrect):** While the Ministry of Finance oversees the NIF, professional Asset Management Companies (AMCs) are entrusted with managing the fund’s corpus to achieve optimal returns.

- **Maintained within Consolidated Fund (Incorrect):** The NIF is maintained outside the Consolidated Fund of India. This provides more flexibility in managing the fund and investing in diverse assets.

- **Appointed Asset Management Companies (Correct):** Selected public sector AMCs are appointed to manage the NIF corpus based on their expertise and track record.

- **Funding Social Sectors (Correct):** A specific portion of the NIF’s annual income is allocated for financing social sector schemes in areas like education, health, and employment.
