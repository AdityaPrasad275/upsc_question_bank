### Question 28
**6] Which one of the following statements is correct? Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act (FRBM
A) concerns: [2006]**


a) Fiscal deficit only

b) Revenue deficit only

c) Both fiscal and revenue deficit

d) Neither fiscal deficit nor revenue deficit.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both fiscal and revenue deficit

**Explanation:**
- The Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act (FRBM
A), enacted in 2003, aims to achieve fiscal consolidation in India. This means promoting responsible fiscal management by controlling government deficits.

The FRBMA deals with **both fiscal deficit and revenue deficit**:

- **Fiscal deficit:** This occurs when the government’s total expenditure exceeds its total revenue (including borrowings).

- **Revenue deficit:** This is a narrower concept where government’s revenue falls short of its non-debt expenditure.

- The FRBM Act sets targets for reducing both these deficits over time. It aims to bring greater discipline and transparency to government spending.
