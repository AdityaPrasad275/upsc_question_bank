### Question 33
**11] With reference to the Indian Public Finance consider the following statements: [2002]**

1. External liabilities reported in Union Budget are based on historical exchange rates
2. The continued high borrowing has kept the real interest rates high in the economy
3. The upward trend in the ratio of Fiscal Deficit to GDP in recent years has an adverse effect to private investments.
4. Interest payments is the single largest component of the non-plan revenue expenditure of the Union Government.

Which of these statements are correct?

a) 1, 2 and 3 
b) 1 and 4

c) 2, 3 and 4 
d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 2, 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
**External liabilities reported in Union Budget are based on historical exchange rates(Incorrect)**

- External liabilities in the Union Budget are typically reported at current exchange rates. This provides a more accurate picture of the outstanding debt burden.

**The continued high borrowing has kept the real interest rates high in the economy****(Correct)**

- When the government borrows heavily, it competes with private borrowers for loanable funds. This can drive up interest rates in the economy, potentially discouraging private investment.

**The upward trend in the ratio of Fiscal Deficit to GDP in recent years has an adverse effect to private investments.****(Correct)**

- A high fiscal deficit can crowd out private investment. When the government borrows a large portion of available funds, there’s less money available for private businesses to invest. This can hinder economic growth.

**Interest payments is the single largest component of the non-plan revenue expenditure of the Union Government.(Correct)**

- Interest payments on past borrowings are a significant expense for the government. This can divert resources away from other spending priorities, such as infrastructure or social programs.
