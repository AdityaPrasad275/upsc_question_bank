a) I-C, II-A, III-B, IV-D

b) I-D, II-C, III-B, IV-A

c) I-A, II-C, III-B, IV-D

d) I-C, II-A, III-D, IV-B

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) I-C, II-A, III-B, IV-D

**Explanation:**
I. Fiscal deficit: This represents the excess of total government expenditure over total government revenue, excluding borrowings. It reflects the government’s borrowing requirements to finance its activities.

II. Budget deficit: This is the excess of total government expenditure over total government revenue. It includes both revenue and capital expenditures but does not consider borrowings.

III. Revenue deficit: This is the excess of revenue expenditure (day-to-day expenses) over revenue receipts (income). It indicates the shortfall in meeting recurring expenses from regular income sources.

IV. Primary deficit: This is the fiscal deficit minus interest payments. It represents the government’s borrowing requirement excluding interest payments on past debts.

Understanding these terms is crucial for assessing the financial health and sustainability of a government’s budgetary policies.
