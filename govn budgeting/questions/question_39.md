### Question 39
**17] Economic Survey in India is published officially, every year by the: [1998]**


a) Reserve Bank of India

b) Planning Commission of India

c) Ministry of Finance, Government of India

d) Ministry of Industries, Government of India

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Ministry of Finance, Government of India

**Explanation:**
The Economic Survey is an annual document published by the Ministry of Finance, Government of India, usually just before the presentation of the Union Budget in the Parliament. It provides an overview of the country’s economic performance over the past year, along with forecasts and analysis of various economic indicators.

Here’s a brief explanation of the role and significance of the Economic Survey:

- **Overview of Economic Performance**: The Economic Survey presents a comprehensive analysis of the country’s economic performance, including growth trends, inflation rates, fiscal deficit, current account deficit, and other key macroeconomic indicators.

- **Policy Recommendations**: It offers insights into the challenges facing the economy and provides policy recommendations to address them. These recommendations often serve as inputs for the government’s policy-making process, including the formulation of the Union Budget.

- **Sectoral Analysis**: The Economic Survey typically includes in-depth analysis of specific sectors such as agriculture, industry, services, infrastructure, and social sectors. This analysis helps policymakers understand sectoral trends and challenges better.

- **International Comparisons**: It may also include comparisons with global economic trends and analyses of international best practices to provide context for India’s economic performance.

- **Data Repository**: The Economic Survey serves as a valuable repository of economic data and statistics, providing researchers, policymakers, and the public with access to reliable and up-to-date information on various aspects of the economy.
