### Question 4
**With reference to Union Budget, consider the following statements: [2024]

**1. The Union Finance Minister on behalf of the Prime Minister lays the Annual Financial Statement before both the Houses of Parliament.
2. At the Union level, no demand for a grant can be made except on the recommendation of the President of India.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2

d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- Art 112 (1) “The President shall in respect of every financial year **cause** to be laid before both the Houses of Parliament a statement of the estimated receipts and expenditure of the Government of India for that year, in this Part referred to as the “annual financial statement”.”

- Above articles gives impression that Finance Minister lays the Annual Financial Statement on behalf of President and / or Govt. of India and not prime minister. **However, since PM is the head of the government, statement 1 cannot be said to be false either. Thus, statement 1 is correct.**

- Acc. To Art 113 (3) No demand for a grant shall be made except on the recommendation of the President. **Thus, second statement is correct.**

- Thus, 
c) Both 1 and 2 is the correct answer.
