### Question 40
**18] Which of the following come under Non-plan expenditure? [1997]**

I. Subsidies
II. Interest payments
III. Defence expenditure
IV. Maintenance expenditure for the infrastructure created in the previous plans Choose the correct answer using the codes given below:

Codes:

a) I and II 
b) I and III

c) II and IV 
d) I, II, III and IV

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) I, II, III and IV

**Explanation:**
I. Subsidies: Subsidies are typically categorized as non-plan expenditure because they are aimed at providing financial support to specific sectors or groups without directly contributing to planned development programs.

II. Interest payments: Interest payments on government borrowings constitute a significant portion of non-plan expenditure as they are recurring expenses and do not contribute to planned development activities.

III. Defence expenditure: Defence expenditure, which includes spending on military personnel, equipment, and operations, is also considered non-plan expenditure as it is not directly related to planned development programs.

IV. Maintenance expenditure for the infrastructure created in the previous plans: Maintenance expenditure for infrastructure created in previous plans is categorized as non-plan expenditure because it involves the upkeep and maintenance of existing infrastructure rather than funding new development projects.
