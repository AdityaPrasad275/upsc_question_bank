### Question 42
**20] Which of the following are among the non-plan expenditures of the Government of India? [1995]**

I. Defence expenditure
II. Subsidies
III. All expenditures linked with the previous plan periods
IV. Interest payment

Choose the correct answer from the codes given below:
Codes:

a) I and II 
b) I and III

c) II and IV 
d) I, II, III and IV

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) I, II, III and IV

**Explanation:**
- **I. Defence expenditure:** While crucial for national security, military spending is considered an ongoing expense, not directly tied to specific development plans.

- **II. Subsidies:** Government outlays for food, fuel, fertilizers, etc., are ongoing expenses and not for new development projects.

- **III. All expenditures linked with the previous plan periods:** Upkeep and maintenance of infrastructure created in past plans are essential but not part of a new development initiative.

- **IV. Interest payment:** Interest on past borrowings is a recurring expense, not related to financing new development projects.
