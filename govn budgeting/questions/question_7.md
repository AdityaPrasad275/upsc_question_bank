### Question 7
**2] In the context of the Indian economy, non-financial debt includes which of the following? [2020]**

1. Housing loans owed by households
2. Amounts outstanding on credit cards
3. Treasury bills
Select the correct answer using the code given below:


a) 1 only

b) 1 and 2 only

c) 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- **Non-financial debt** refers to the total amount owed by entities outside the financial sector. This includes individuals, businesses, and the government.

- **Housing loans owed by households:** When a household takes a housing loan, they accumulate debt with a bank or financial institution (non-financial entity).

- **Amounts outstanding on credit cards:** Similar to housing loans, unpaid credit card balances represent debt owed by individuals to banks (non-financial entities).

- **Treasury bills:** These are short-term debt instruments issued by the government (non-financial entity) to borrow money.

Therefore, all three options contribute to non-financial debt in the Indian economy.
