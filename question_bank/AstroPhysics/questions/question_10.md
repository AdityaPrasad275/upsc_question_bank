### Question 10
**1] “The experiment will employ a trio of spacecraft flying in formation in the shape of an equilateral triangle that has sides one million kilometres long, with lasers shining between the craft.” The experiment in question refers to: [2020]|
**

a) Voyager-2

b) New horizons

c) Lisa Pathfinder

d) Evolved LISA

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Evolved LISA

**Explanation:**
- **
a) Voyager 2**: Voyager 2 is a space probe launched by NASA in 1977 as part of the Voyager program. It has provided invaluable data about the outer planets of our solar system, including close flybys of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have visited Uranus and Neptune. After completing its primary mission, Voyager 2 continues to operate and is now in interstellar space, making it one of the farthest human-made objects from Earth.

- **
b) New Horizons**: New Horizons is a NASA space probe launched in 2006 with the primary mission of studying Pluto and its moons. In 2015, it made a historic flyby of Pluto, providing the first detailed images of the dwarf planet and its moons. After its encounter with Pluto, New Horizons continued its journey into the Kuiper Belt, where it conducted a flyby of the Kuiper Belt object Arrokoth in 2019. New Horizons has greatly expanded our understanding of the outer solar system and continues to explore the distant reaches of space.

- **
c) LISA Pathfinder**: LISA Pathfinder was a European Space Agency (ES
A) mission launched in 2015 to demonstrate key technologies for the future LISA (Laser Interferometer Space Antenn
a) mission. Its primary goal was to test the concept of free-falling test masses in space, which are crucial for detecting gravitational waves. LISA Pathfinder successfully demonstrated the feasibility of this technology and paved the way for the development of the LISA mission.

- **
d) Evolved LISA**: Evolved LISA (eLIS
A) is a proposed space mission by ESA and NASA, intended to succeed the LISA Pathfinder mission. It aims to detect and study gravitational waves from space using a constellation of spacecraft in a triangular formation separated by millions of kilometers. Lasers are used to measure the distances between the spacecraft with extreme precision, allowing scientists to detect and study gravitational waves in space. Evolved LISA will build upon the success of LISA Pathfinder and is expected to provide unprecedented insights into astrophysical phenomena such as black hole mergers and the early universe. The planned launched date for the project is year 2035.
