### Question 16
**4] Consider the following phenomenon: [2018]
**
1. Light is affected by gravity.
2. The universe is constantly expanding.
3. Matter wraps its surrounding space- time.

Which of the above is/are prediction/predictions of Albert Einstein’s General Theory Of Relativity, often discussed in media?

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Albert Einstein’s General Theory of Relativity fundamentally changed our understanding of gravity, describing it not as a force but as a curvature of space-time caused by mass and energy.

- This curvature affects the path of light, causing it to bend around massive objects, a phenomenon known as gravitational lensing.

- The theory also predicts that the universe is constantly expanding, a concept later confirmed by the observation of redshift in distant galaxies, indicating that they are moving away from us.

- Additionally, matter wraps or distorts its surrounding space-time, influencing the motion of other objects. This wrapping effect explains why planets orbit stars and why light bends near massive objects.

- **Thus, all the three statements are true.**
