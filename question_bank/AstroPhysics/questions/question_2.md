**2] ****Consider the following space missions:** **[2025/A-83]

**I. Axiom-4
II. SpaDeX
III. Gaganyaan
How many of the space missions given above encourage and support micro-gravity research?


a) Only one

b) Only two

c) All the three

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) All the three

**Explanation:**
- **Statement I: Correct** – Axiom-4 was a private space mission to the International Space Station that supported microgravity research and experiments.

- **Statement II: Correct** – SpaDeX (Space Docking Experiment) by ISRO involved microgravity testing as part of its in-orbit docking and related research objectives.

- **Statement III: Correct** – Gaganyaan, India’s human spaceflight mission, includes provisions for conducting microgravity experiments in its crew module.

- Hence, all three missions support microgravity research. Correct Ans: 
c) ) All the three
