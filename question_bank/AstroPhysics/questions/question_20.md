### Question 20
**1] What is ‘Greased Lightning-10 (GL-10)’, recently in the news? [2016]
**

a) Electric plane tested by NASA

b) Solar-powered two-seater aircraft designed by Japan

c) Space observatory launched by China

d) Reusable rocket designed by ISRO

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Electric plane tested by NASA

**Explanation:**
- The Greased Lightning-10 (GL-10) is an experimental, hybrid-electric, tilt-wing aircraft developed by NASA. It is a small, unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) designed to demonstrate the capabilities and advantages of electric propulsion and vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL).

- The GL-10 has 10 electric motors that allow it to take off and land like a helicopter and then transition to horizontal flight like a conventional airplane.

- This technology aims to enhance the efficiency, flexibility, and performance of future aircraft, potentially leading to advances in personal air vehicles, delivery drones, and other applications in aviation.

- Thus, option 
a) is correct answer.
