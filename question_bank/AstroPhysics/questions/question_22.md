### Question 22
**3] Consider the following statements: [2016]
**
The Mangalyaan launched by ISRO
1. Is also called the Mars Orbiter Mission
2. Made India the second country to have a spacecraft orbit the Mars after USA
3. Made India the only country to be successful in making its spacecraft orbit the Mars in its very first attempt

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- The Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) launched by ISRO has several notable achievements:

- **First Attempt Success:** Mangalyaan made India the first country to successfully reach Mars on its maiden attempt in 2014.

- **Low-Cost Mission:** It was one of the most cost-effective missions to Mars, with a budget of approximately $74 million.

- **India became the fourth country**/space agency to have a spacecraft orbit Mars after the Soviet Union, the USA, and the European Space Agency (ES
A).

- **Longest Continuously Operating Mars Mission:** It remained operational for 8 years and 8 days (2014-2022)

- **Thus, only 1st and 3rd statements are correct.**
