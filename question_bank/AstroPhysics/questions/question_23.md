### Question 23
**1] In which of the following activities are Indian Remote Sensing (IRS) satellites used? [2015]
**
1. Assessment of crop productivity
2. Locating ground water resources
3. Mineral exploration
4. Telecommunications
5. Traffic studies

Select the correct answer using the code given below

a) 1, 2 and 3 only

b) 4 and 5 only

c) 1 and 2 only

d) 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1, 2 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Indian Remote Sensing (IRS) satellites are typically placed in low Earth orbit (LEO).

- These are specifically designed and equipped with sensors and instruments to capture detailed imagery and data across various spectral bands, making them ideal for activities like assessing crop productivity, locating groundwater resources, and mineral exploration.

- Communication satellites are placed in geostationary orbits and have different design specifications and technologies compared to IRS satellites. Thus, IRS satellites cannot be used for telecommunication purpose.

- For effective traffic studies, continuous and real-time monitoring is often necessary. While LEO satellites can provide high-resolution images, they pass over the same location only at specific intervals (every few days), which is not frequent enough for real-time traffic management. Ground-based sensors and other technologies can provide the continuous, real-time data required for this purpose.

- **Thus, only first three purposes mentioned are correct.**
