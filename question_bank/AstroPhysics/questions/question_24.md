### Question 24
**2] The term ‘Goldilocks Zone’ is often seen in the news in the context of: [2015]**


a) The limits of habitable zone above the surface of the Earth

b) Regions inside the Earth-like planets in outer space

c) Search for the Earth-like planets in outer space

d) Search for meteorites containing precious metals

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Search for the Earth-like planets in outer space

**Explanation:**
- The “Goldilocks Zone” refers to the habitable zone around a star where conditions are just right for liquid water to exist on a planet’s surface. This concept is named after the “Goldilocks” fairy tale, where Goldilocks finds a porridge that’s neither too hot nor too cold, but just right.

- Our planet is in the Sun’s Goldilocks Zone, allowing for the presence of liquid water and life.

- Scientists search for exoplanets (planets outside our solar system) within the Goldilocks Zones of their respective stars to find potential habitable worlds.
