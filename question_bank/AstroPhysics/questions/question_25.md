### Question 25
**1] Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched? Spacecraft Purpose: [2014]
**
1. Cassini-Huygens: Orbiting the Venus and transmitting data to the Earth
2. Messenger: Mapping and investigating
3. Voyager 1 and 2: Exploring the outer solar system

Select the correct answer using the code given below.

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Cassini-Huygens was a joint NASA-ESA mission designed to study the planet Saturn and its moons. The Cassini spacecraft orbited Saturn for over 13 years, conducting numerous flybys of Saturn’s moons, particularly Titan, which was the primary target for the Huygens probe.

- The Huygens probe, built by ESA, detached from Cassini and successfully landed on Titan, becoming the first spacecraft to land on a moon in the outer solar system. **Thus, 1st statement is not correct.**

- MESSENGER (MErcury Surface, Space ENvironment, GEochemistry, and Ranging) was a NASA mission to study the planet Mercury, the smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System. The spacecraft MESSENGER orbited Mercury for over four years, conducting detailed observations of the planet’s surface, composition, magnetic field, and exosphere. The description given in the question i.e. mapping and investing is too generic, and thus can be applied in this case as well. **Hence 2nd statement is correct.**

- The Voyager program was an American scientific program that employed **two interstellar probes**, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2. They were launched in 1977. As of 2024, the Voyagers are still in operation beyond the outer boundary of the heliosphere in interstellar space. They collect and transmit useful data to Earth. **Thus, 3rd statement is correct.**
