### Question 26
**1] Which of the following is/are cited by the scientists as evidence/ evidences for the ‘continued expansion of universe? [2012]**

1. Detection of microwaves in space
2. Observation of redshift phenomenon in space
3. Movement of asteroids in space
4. Occurrence of supernova explosions in space

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

a) 1 and 2 
b) 2 Only

c) 1,3 &amp; 4 
d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- Scientists cite several pieces of evidence for the continued expansion of the universe. It includes:

- **Cosmic microwave background radiation:** The CMB radiation, leftover from the early universe, shows slight temperature variations that support the theory of an expanding universe.

- **Redshift of galaxies:** The light from distant galaxies appears redder than expected, indicating that they are moving away from us, consistent with an expanding universe.

- **Hubble’s Law:** Edwin Hubble observed that the farther a galaxy is from us, the faster it appears to be moving away, providing direct evidence for the expansion of the universe.

- Movement of asteroids does not talk much about the expansion of universe.

- Similarly, occurrence of supernova explosions also does not tell much about expansion of universe

- **Thus, only 1st and 2nd statements are correct.**
