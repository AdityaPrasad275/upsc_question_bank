### Question 29
**2] An artificial satellite orbiting around the earth does not fall down. This is so because the attraction of earth? [2011]**


a) Does not exist at such distance.

b) Is neutralized by the attraction of the moon.

c) Provides the necessary speed for its steady motion.

d) Provides the necessary acceleration for its motion.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Provides the necessary acceleration for its motion.

**Explanation:**
- An artificial satellite orbiting around the Earth does not fall down because it is continuously moving forward with sufficient velocity.

- The gravitational attraction of the Earth provides the necessary acceleration for the motion of satellite. This acceleration due to gravity acts as the centripetal force that keeps the satellite in its orbit. Without this gravitational pull, objects in orbit would not stay in motion around the Earth and would instead travel in a straight line tangential to the Earth’s surface.

- Thus, statement 
d) is correct answer.
