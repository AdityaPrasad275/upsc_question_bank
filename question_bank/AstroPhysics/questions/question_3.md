### Question 3
**3] ****With reference to monoclonal antibodies, often mentioned in news, consider the following statements:** **[2025/A-48]

**I. They are man-made proteins.
II. They stimulate immunological function due to their ability to bind to specific antigens.
III. They are used in treating viral infections like that of Nipah virus.
Which of the statements given above are correct?


a) I and II only

b) II and III only

c) I and III only

d) I, II and III

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) I, II and III

**Explanation:**
- **Statement I: Correct** – Monoclonal antibodies are laboratory-produced proteins designed to mimic natural antibodies.

- **Statement II: Correct** – They work by specifically binding to target antigens, thereby stimulating immunological function. enhancing immune responses.

- **Statement III: Correct** – Monoclonal antibodies have been used experimentally to treat viral infections, including Nipah virus, by neutralising the pathogen.

- Hence, all three statements are correct. Correct Ans: 
d) ) I, II and III
