### Question 32
**2] Consider the following statements: The satellite Oceansat – 2 launched by India helps in: [2010]**

1. Estimating the water vapour content in the atmosphere.
2. Predicting the onset of monsoons.
3. Monitoring the pollution of coastal waters

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 and 2 only 
b) 2 only

c) 1 and 3 only 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Oceansat-2 was launched by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) on September 23, 2009. The satellite was placed into orbit by the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV-C14) from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota. Oceansat-2 is a follow-up to the earlier Oceansat-1, and it carries instruments like the Ocean Colour Monitor (OCM-2) and the Scatterometer to fulfill its mission objectives related to ocean and atmospheric studies.

- The satellite aids in ocean surface monitoring, weather forecasting, fisheries management, disaster management, and climate studies. It tracks sea surface temperature, ocean color, and phytoplankton concentrations, improves monsoon predictions and weather forecasts, identifies potential fishing zones, monitors natural disasters like cyclones and tsunamis, and contributes to climate change research by providing long-term ocean data.

- **Thus, 
d) is correct answer.**
