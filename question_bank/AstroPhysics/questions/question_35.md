### Question 35
**5] Which one of the following planets has largest number of natural satellites or moons? [2009]**


a) Jupiter 
b) Mars

c) Saturn 
d) Venus

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Jupiter

**Explanation:**
- As of the latest data **(June 2023), **here is a list of the known number of natural satellites (moons) for each planet in our Solar System:

- Mercury: 0 moons

- Venus: 0 moons

- Earth: 1 moon (the Moon)

- Mars: 2 moons (Phobos and Deimos)

- Jupiter: 95 moons

- **Saturn: 146 moons**

- Uranus: 27 moons

- Neptune: 14 moons

- These numbers can change (do keep changing) as new moons are discovered and classified by astronomers.
