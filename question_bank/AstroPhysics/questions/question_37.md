### Question 37
**7] ISRO successfully conducted a rocket test using cryogenic engines in the year 2007. Where is the test-stand used for the purpose, located? [2008]**


a) Balasoren 
b) thiruvananthapuram

c) Mahendragiri 
d) Karwar

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Mahendragiri

**Explanation:**
- ISRO successfully conducted a rocket test using cryogenic engines in the year 2007. The test-stand used for this purpose is located at the ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPR
C) in Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu, India.
