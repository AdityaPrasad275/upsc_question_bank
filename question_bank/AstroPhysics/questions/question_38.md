### Question 38
**8] Consider the following statements in respect of a jet engine and a rocket: [2008]**

1. A jet engine uses the surrounding air for its oxygen supply and so is Unsutable for motion in space
2. A rocket carries its own supply of oxygen in the gas form, and fuel.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- Jet engines, such as those used in airplanes, rely on the intake of air from the atmosphere to mix with fuel for combustion. In space, where there is no atmosphere, there is no air for the jet engine to intake and, therefore, no oxygen supply for combustion. Thus, traditional jet engines are indeed unsuitable for propulsion in space.

- Rockets are designed to carry both fuel and oxidizer onboard in separate tanks. The fuel and oxidizer are combined and ignited in the combustion chamber to produce the thrust necessary for propulsion. Unlike jet engines, rockets do not rely on atmospheric oxygen for combustion. Instead, they carry their own oxidizer, usually in the form of liquid oxygen or other oxidizing agents, which allows them to operate in the vacuum of space.

- Thus, both statements are correct.
