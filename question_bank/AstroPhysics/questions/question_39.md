### Question 39
**9] What is the purpose of the US Space Agency’s Themis Mission, which was recently in the news? [2008]**


a) To study the possibility of life on Mars

b) To study the satellites of Saturn

c) To study the Colourful display of high latitude skies

d) To build a Space laboratory to study the stellar explosions

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) To study the Colourful display of high latitude skies

**Explanation:**
- The purpose of the US Space Agency’s THEMIS (Time History of Events and Macroscale Interactions during Substorms) mission is to study the dynamic behavior of the Earth’s magnetosphere and its interactions with the solar wind.

- Specifically, THEMIS aims to investigate the process of magnetic reconnection, which occurs when the magnetic field lines of the Earth and the solar wind interact and release energy.

- By understanding these processes, THEMIS contributes to our knowledge of space weather phenomena, such as geomagnetic storms and substorms, which can impact satellites, power grids, and communication systems on Earth.

- THEMIS aims to uncover the dynamics of the Earth’s magnetosphere and how it interacts with solar wind. Thus, part of THEMIS’s mission includes studying the colorful display of high-latitude skies as a way to understand the underlying processes causing these phenomena.

- So the correct answer is 
c)
