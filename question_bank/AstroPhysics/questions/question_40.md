d) USA

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) European Union 
b) Japan

**Explanation:**
- Apophis: Apophis is not a spacecraft; it is **an asteroid** that gained attention due to its close approaches to Earth.

- **Cassini: Cassini is a spacecraft that was part of the Cassini-Huygens mission**, a collaboration between NASA, ESA, and ASI, which studied Saturn and its system.

- Spitzer: Launched in 2003, NASA’s Spitzer Space Telescope was the fourth and final addition to NASA’s Great Observatory program**. Spitzer is a space telescope**, designed to study the universe in infrared light.

- TechSar: TechSar (also known as TecSAR or Polaris) **is an Israeli reconnaissance satellite**, not a spacecraft in the context of space exploration missions like Cassini or Spitzer.
