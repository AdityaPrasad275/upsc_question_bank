### Question 42
**12] Which one of the following pairs is not correctly matched? [2007]**


a) Cosmic Background Explore(COBE) – Satellite programme

b) Falcon – Under-sea cable system

c) Discovery – Space Shutle

d) Atlantis – Space Station

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Falcon – Under-sea cable system

**Explanation:**
- **The Cosmic Background Explorer (COBE)** was a NASA mission launched in 1989 to study the cosmic microwave background radiation (CM
B) of the universe. The COBE satellite was equipped with three primary scientific instruments (FIRAS, DMR, and DIRBE) to measure the cosmic microwave background radiation (CM
B) and the infrared background.

- **Falcon** typically refers to SpaceX’s Falcon family of rockets, which includes Falcon 1, Falcon 9, and Falcon Heavy. The Falcon rockets are known for their innovative design, featuring reusable first stages that significantly reduce launch costs. Falcon 9, the most widely used in the series, has been instrumental in deploying satellites, resupplying the International Space Station (ISS), and launching crewed missions under NASA’s Commercial Crew Program.

- **Discovery** is one of NASA’s space shuttles, part of the Space Shuttle program. It flew its maiden voyage in 1984 and completed 39 missions before its retirement in 2011. Discovery’s notable missions include deploying the Hubble Space Telescope, conducting scientific experiments, and assembling parts of the ISS. It also played a key role in returning the shuttle fleet to flight after the Challenger and Columbia disasters.

- **Atlantis** is another space shuttle in NASA’s Space Shuttle program, making its first flight in 1985 and concluding with its final mission in 2011. Atlantis conducted 33 missions, contributing to numerous space explorations, including deploying planetary probes like Magellan and Galileo, delivering vital components to the ISS, and participating in the last servicing mission to the Hubble Space Telescope. Atlantis’s final mission, STS-135, marked the end of the Space Shuttle program.

- Thus, only pair b is not correctly matched.
