### Question 43
**13] NASA’S Deep Impact space mission was employed to take detailed pictures of which comet nucleus? [2007]**


a) halley’s Comet 
b) Hale-Bopp

c) Hyakutake 
d) Tempel 1

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Tempel 1

**Explanation:**
- NASA’s Deep Impact space mission was employed to take detailed pictures of the nucleus of comet **Tempel 1**. The mission, launched on January 12, 2005, successfully impacted the comet on July 4, 2005. The resulting collision provided valuable data and high-resolution images, revealing the comet’s composition, structure, and the properties of its nucleus.
