### Question 45
**15] What is the average distance (approximate) between the Sun and the Earth /: [2007]**


a) 70×105km 
b) 100×105km

c) 110×106km 
d) 150×106km

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 150×106km

**Explanation:**
- The average distance between the Sun and the Earth is approximately **150 million kilometers (150×106km).** This distance is often referred to as one astronomical unit (AU), which is a commonly used unit of measurement in astronomy for distances within the Solar System.
