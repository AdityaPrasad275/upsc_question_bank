### Question 47
**17] Which one of the following pairs is not correctly matched? Recent Scientific Achievement: Country: [2006]**


a) Creating the World’s first mouse: United Kingdom

b) Cloning a human embryo for the first time: Germany

c) Guiding a spacecraft for Collision with a comet: United States of America

d) Landing a spacecraft on an asteroid: Japan

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Cloning a human embryo for the first time: Germany

**Explanation:**
- The first mouse was cloned in the United Kingdom by scientists at the Roslin Institute in Edinburgh in 1997.

- The first cloning of a human embryo was not conducted in Germany. Instead, it occurred in the United States. **The first successful human embryo cloning was reported by scientists at Advanced Cell Technology (ACT) in Massachusetts, USA, in 2001.**

- NASA’s Deep Impact mission, which involved guiding a spacecraft to collide with the comet Tempel 1, was conducted by the United States. It was first such an instance.

- Japan’s space agency, JAXA, successfully landed the Hayabusa spacecraft on the asteroid Itokawa in 2005. It was the first landing of a spacecraft on an asteroid.

- Thus, pair 
b) is not correctly matched.
