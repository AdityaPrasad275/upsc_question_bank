### Question 49
**19] Consider the following statements: [2005]**

1. A geostationary satellite is at an approximate height of 10000 km.
2. FM transmission of music is of very good quality because the atmospheric or man-made noises which are generally frequency variations can do little harm.

Which of the statements is/are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 only

**Explanation:**
- A geostationary satellite orbits the Earth at an approximate height of 35,786 kilometers (22,236 miles) above the equator.

- FM (Frequency Modulation) transmission provides very good quality music because it is less susceptible to noise and interference. The primary reason is that most atmospheric or man-made noises are amplitude variations, which have little effect on FM signals, as FM is based on varying the frequency of the carrier wave.

- Thus, only 2nd statement is true.
