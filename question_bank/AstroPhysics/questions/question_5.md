### Question 5
**1] Consider the following pairs: [2023]**

Objects in space: Description
1. Cepheids : Giant clouds of dust and gas in space
2. Nebulae : Stars which brighten and dim periodically
3. Pulsars : Neutron stars that are formed when massive stars run out of fuel and collapse

How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

a) Only one

b) Only two

c) All three

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Only one

**Explanation:**
- **Cepheids** are a type of variable star that pulsates in a predictable pattern, varying in both brightness and size. These pulsations occur over a regular cycle, which can range from a few days to months.

- A **nebula** is a vast cloud of gas and dust in space. These formations are often the birthplaces or remnants of stars.

- **Pulsars** are highly magnetized, rotating neutron stars that emit beams of electromagnetic radiation. Pulsars are formed from the remnants of massive stars that have undergone supernova explosions. The core left behind collapses into a neutron star.

- **Thus, only third pair is correctly matched.**
