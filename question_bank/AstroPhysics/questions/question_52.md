### Question 52
**22] Among the following which planet takes maximum time for one revolution around the Sun? [2003]**


a) Earth 
b) Jupiter

c) Mars 
d) Venus

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Jupiter

**Explanation:**
- Here the list of the time taken by each planet in our solar system to complete one revolution around the Sun, also known as their orbital period:

- **Mercury:** Approximately 88 Earth days

- **Venus:** Approximately 225 Earth days

- **Earth:** Approximately 365.25 Earth days

- **Mars:** Approximately 687 Earth days

- **Jupiter:** Approximately 11.86 Earth years

- **Saturn:** Approximately 29.46 Earth years

- **Uranus:** Approximately 84 Earth years

- **Neptune:** Approximately 164.8 Earth years

- **Thus, among the given options, Jupiter is correct.**
