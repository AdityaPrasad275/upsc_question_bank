### Question 54
**24] Which one of the following statements is correct with reference to our solar system? [2002]**


a) The Earth is the densest of all the planets in our solar system

b) The predominant element in the composition of Earth is silicon

c) The Sun contains 75 per cent of the mass of the solar system

d) The diameter of the Sun is 190 times that of the Earth

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) The Earth is the densest of all the planets in our solar system

**Explanation:**
- 
a) The densest planet in our solar system is Earth, with an average density of approximately 5.515 grams per cubic centimetre (g/cm³).

- 
b) The mass of the Earth is approximately 5.97×1024 kg. In bulk, by mass, it is composed mostly of iron (32.1%), oxygen (30.1%), silicon (15.1%), magnesium (13.9%)

- 
c) The Sun actually contains approximately 99.8% of the mass of the entire solar system. The remaining 0.2% of the solar system’s mass is distributed among all the planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other objects orbiting the Sun.

- 
d) The Sun’s diameter is 1,392,000 km, and Earth’s is 12,742 km. So the Sun’s diameter is approximately 109 times that of earth.

- Thus, only statement 
a) is correct.
