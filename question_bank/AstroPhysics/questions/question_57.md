### Question 57
**27] Assertion (
A): Artificial satellites are always launched from the earth in the eastward direction.: [2002]**

Reason (R): The earth rotates from west to east and so the satellite attains the escape velocity.

a) Both A and R are individually true and R is the correct explanation of A

b) Both A and R are individually true but R is not a correct explanation of A

c) A is true but R is false

d) A is false but R is true

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) A is true but R is false

**Explanation:**
- Artificial satellites are launched from the Earth in the eastward direction to take advantage of the Earth’s rotation and conserve fuel, making it more efficient and cost-effective to place satellites into orbit. **Thus, assertion is correct.**

- While its true that the rotation of earth from west to east help propel satellites into orbit, it is not responsible for satellites attaining escape velocity. **Hence Reason is false.**
