### Question 59
**29] The location of the space organisation units have been marked in the given map as 1, 2, 3 and 4. Match these units with the list given and select the correct answer using the codes given below the Lists: [2001]**

A. ISRO
B. IIRS
C. NRSA
D. SAC

Codes:
 A B C D

a) 4 1 2 3

b) 1 4 3 2

c) 1 4 2 3

d) 4 1 3 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 4 1 3 2

**Explanation:**
- ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation): ISRO’s headquarters is located in Bengaluru (formerly known as Bangalore), Karnataka, and it has various centers and facilities across the country.

- IIRS (Indian Institute of Remote Sensing): IIRS is located in Dehradun, the capital city of Uttarakhand state, in northern India. It is a premier institute for education, research, and training in remote sensing and geoinformatics.

- NRSA (National Remote Sensing Centre): NRSA is situated in Hyderabad, the capital city of Telangana state, in southern India. It is responsible for satellite data acquisition and processing for various applications.

- SAC (Space Applications Centre): SAC is located in Ahmedabad, the largest city in Gujarat state, western India. It is one of the major centers of ISRO and is involved in the development of satellite technology and applications.
