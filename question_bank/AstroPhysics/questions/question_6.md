### Question 6
**2]** **Which one of the following countries has its own Satellite Navigation System? [2023]**


a) Australia

b) Canada

c) Israel

d) Japan

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Japan

**Explanation:**
- **Japan has its own satellite navigation system known as the Quasi-Zenith Satellite System (QZSS).** It is designed to enhance the accuracy of GPS in the region and provide better coverage for users in Japan and the surrounding area.

- All other countries mentioned in the options rely on the GPS of the United States.

- **Only following countries in world have their own Satellite Navigation System:**

- United States: Global Positioning System (GPS)

- Russia: GLONASS (Global Navigation Satellite System)

- European Union: Galileo

- China: BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS)

- India: NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation), also known as the Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System (IRNSS)

- Japan: Quasi-Zenith Satellite System (QZSS)
