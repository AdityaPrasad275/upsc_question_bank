### Question 64
**34] One Astronomical Unit is the average distance between: [1998]**


a) Earth and the Sun

b) Earth and the Moon

c) Jupiter and the Sun

d) Pluto and the Sun

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Earth and the Sun

**Explanation:**
- One Astronomical Unit (AU) is the average distance from the Earth to the Sun. It is used as a standard unit of measurement in astronomy for distances within our solar system.

- One AU is about 149.6 million kilometres
