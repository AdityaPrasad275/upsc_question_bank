### Question 69
**39] Which one of the following satellites is to be launched from India in 1996? [1996]**


a) IRS-P2 
b) IRS-P3

c) IRS-EN 
d) IRS-ID

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) IRS-P3

**Explanation:**
- The Indian Remote Sensing Satellite (IRS) series is a group of Earth observation satellites launched by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

- IRS-P3 was launched in March 1996, primarily for remote sensing applications, including agriculture, forestry, water resources management, and urban planning.
