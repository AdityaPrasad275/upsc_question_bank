### Question 8
**2] Which one of the following statements best reflects the idea behind the “Fractional Orbital Bombardment System” often talked about in the media? [2022]
**

a) A hypersonic missile is launched into space to counter the asteroid approaching the Earth and explode it in space.

b) A spacecraft lands on another planet after making several orbital motions.

c) A missile is put into a stable orbit around the Earth and deorbits over a target on the Earth.

d) A spacecraft moves along a comet with the same speed and places a probe on its surface

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) A missile is put into a stable orbit around the Earth and deorbits over a target on the Earth.

**Explanation:**
- The Fractional Orbital Bombardment System (FOBS) is a concept for a nuclear weapon delivery system that was developed during the Cold War.

- FOBS involves launching a nuclear warhead into a low Earth orbit, allowing it to travel partway around the Earth before re-entering the atmosphere to hit its target. Unlike traditional intercontinental ballistic missiles (ICBMs) that follow a high, predictable arc, FOBS can approach a target from unexpected directions, making it harder to detect and intercept.

- The system’s name, “Fractional Orbital,” comes from the fact that the warhead does not complete a full orbit around the Earth. Instead, it is de-orbited at a specific point to hit the target.

- **Thus, option c is correct.**
