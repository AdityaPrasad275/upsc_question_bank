### Question 12
**2] Consider the following statements: [2020]
**
1. Genetic changes can be introduced in the cells that produce eggs or sperms of a prospective parent.
2. A person’s genome can be edited before birth at the early embryonic stage.
3. Human induced pluripotent stem cells can be injected into the embryo of a pig.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 2 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- All the statements provided are correct:

- 1) This statement refers to germline gene editing, where genetic modifications are made to the reproductive cells (sperm or eggs) of an individual. These changes can be inherited by future generations.

- 2) This statement describes editing the genome of an embryo at an early stage of development, typically through techniques like CRISPR-Cas9. This approach allows for the correction of genetic mutations or the introduction of desired traits before birth. Although not widespread, it is possible.

- 3) This statement refers to interspecies chimeras, where human cells, such as induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs), are introduced into animal embryos, such as pigs. This technique is used for various research purposes, including studying human development and disease.
