### Question 14
**4] In the context of recent advances of human reproductive technology, “Pronuclear Transfer” is used for: [2020]
**

a) Fertilization of egg in vitro by the donor sperm

b) Genetic modification of sperm producing cells

c) Development of stem cells into functional embryos

d) Prevention of mitochondrial diseases in offspring

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Prevention of mitochondrial diseases in offspring

**Explanation:**
- Pronuclear transfer is a **technique used in reproductive medicine and genetic engineering to prevent the transmission of mitochondrial diseases from mother to child. **It involves transferring the nuclear genetic material from one fertilized egg (zygote) to another egg that has healthy mitochondria.
