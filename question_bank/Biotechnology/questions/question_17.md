### Question 17
**3] What is Cas9 protein that is often mentioned in news? [2019]
**

a) A molecular scissors used in targeted gene editing

b) A biosensor used in the accurate detection of pathogens in patients

c) A gene that makes plants pest-resistant

d) A herbicidal substance synthesized in genetically modified crops

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) A molecular scissors used in targeted gene editing

**Explanation:**
- Cas9 (CRISPR-associated protein 9) is an enzyme that plays a crucial role in the CRISPR (Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats) gene-editing system. **It is widely used in genetic engineering for its ability to make precise cuts in DNA, allowing for targeted gene modifications. Thus, it acts as molecular scissor allowing targeted gene editing.**
