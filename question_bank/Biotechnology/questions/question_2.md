### Question 2
**1] In which of the following are hydrogels used? [2024]

**1. Controlled drug delivery in patients
2. Mobile air-conditioning systems
3. Preparation of industrial lubricants

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

a) 1 only

b) 1 and 2 only

c) 2 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Hydrogels are soft and wet materials with a three-dimensional porous network structure, capable of swelling to retain a large volume of water and maintaining semi-solid integrity.

- Hydrogels are **commonly used in controlled drug delivery systems**. They have the ability to absorb and retain large amounts of water, making them suitable for releasing drugs in a controlled manner over time.

- Air-conditioning relies more on refrigerants and mechanical systems for cooling, not on hydrogel materials. We could not find any evidence linking hydrogel with mobile air-conditioning. 

- Hydrogels can be used in industrial lubricants. They serve as thickeners and stabilizers, giving certain lubricants a desired consistency.

- Thus, first and third statement are true for certain. Second is inconclusive. However, since 1 and 3 only is not a given option, 
d) 1, 2 and 3, is most likely the correct answer.
