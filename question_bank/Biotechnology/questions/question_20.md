### Question 20
**2] What is the application of Somatic Cell Nuclear Transfer Technology? [2017]
**

a) Production of biolarvicides

b) Manufacture of biodegradable plastics

c) Reproductive cloning of animals

d) Production of organisms free of diseases

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Reproductive cloning of animals

**Explanation:**
- Somatic Cell Nuclear Transfer (SCNT) is a **cloning technique** where the nucleus of a somatic cell (a non-reproductive cell) is transferred into an enucleated egg cell (an egg cell from which the nucleus has been remove
d). The resulting cell is stimulated to divide and develop into an embryo, which can then be implanted into a surrogate mother to develop into a clone of the organism that provided the somatic cell nucleus.
