### Question 21
**1] In the context of the developments in Bioinformatics, the term ‘transcriptome’, sometimes seen in the news, refers to: [2016]
**

a) A range of enzymes used in genome editing

b) The full range of mRNA molecules expressed by an organism

c) The description of the mechanism of gene expression

d) A mechanism of genetic mutations taking place in cells

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) The full range of mRNA molecules expressed by an organism

**Explanation:**
- The term “transcriptome” refers to the **complete set of RNA transcripts produced by the genome of an organism at any given time. **This includes all types of RNA molecules, such as messenger RNA (mRN
A), ribosomal RNA (rRN
A), transfer RNA (tRN
A), and non-coding RNAs, that are transcribed from DNA. The transcriptome reflects the genes that are actively being expressed in a cell or tissue under specific conditions and provides insights into gene function and regulation.
