### Question 23
**2] With reference to the use of nanotechnology in health sector, which of the following statements is/are correct? [2015]
**
1. Targeted drug delivery is made possible by nanotechnology
2. Nanotechnology can largely contribute to gene therapy

Select the correct answer using the code given below.

a) 1 only

b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2

d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- Nanotechnology is the science, engineering, and application of materials and devices at the nanoscale, typically ranging from 1 to 100 nanometres. At this scale, materials exhibit unique properties and behaviours that differ from those observed at larger scales.

- Nanotechnology involves manipulating and controlling matter at the atomic or molecular level to create new structures, materials, and devices with novel properties and functionalities.

- **Because of its ability to operate at such a small (nano) scale, it will have wide applications in health sector. Among other things, it can be used in targeted drug delivery as well as in gene therapy.**
