### Question 24
**1] Consider the following techniques/phenomena: [2014]
**
1. Budding and grafting in fruit plants
2. Cytoplasmic male sterility
3. Gene silencing

Which of the above is/are used to create transgenic crops?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Budding and grafting are horticultural techniques used to propagate fruit plants by joining parts of different plants together. **However, these methods are not typically used to create transgenic crops, which involve the introduction of foreign genes into the plant’s genome.**

- Cytoplasmic male sterility (CMS) is a phenomenon where plants are unable to produce functional pollen. It is used in plant breeding programs to facilitate hybrid seed production. CMS lines are often crossed with plants carrying the desired transgenic traits, allowing for the production of hybrid seeds with the desired traits. **Thus, this technique is used to create transgenic crops.**

- **Gene silencing techniques, such as RNA interference (RNAi) or antisense technology, are commonly used to create transgenic crops.** In these methods, specific genes in the plant’s genome are targeted and suppressed or silenced, either to enhance desirable traits or to confer resistance to pests, diseases, or herbicides.
