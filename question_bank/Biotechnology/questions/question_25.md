### Question 25
**1] Recombinant DNA technology (genetic engineering) allows genes to be transferred: [2013]
**
1. Across different species of plants
2. From animals to plants
3. From microorganisms to higher organisms

Select the correct answer using the codes given below.

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Recombinant DNA technology is a technique used to manipulate DNA sequences by combining DNA molecules from different sources. It involves the creation of recombinant DNA molecules, which are formed by joining together DNA fragments from different organisms or synthetic sources. Recombinant DNA technology allows scientists to create novel DNA sequences with specific characteristics or functions.

- **The genes can be transferred in all three cases as mentioned int the question, hence 
d) is the right answer.**
