### Question 26
**2] Mycorrhizal biotechnology has been used in rehabilitating degraded sites because mycorrhiza enables the plants to? [2013]
**
1. Resist drought and increase absorptive area
2. Tolerate extremes of ph
3. Resist disease infestation

Select the correct answer using the codes given below.

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2, and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2, and 3

**Explanation:**
- Mycorrhizal biotechnology involves the application of mycorrhizal fungi in various fields to enhance plant growth, improve soil health, and promote sustainable agriculture.

- Mycorrhizae are symbiotic associations formed between fungi and the roots of most plant species, where the fungi colonize the root system and form a mutually beneficial relationship with the plants.

- Mycorrhizal biotechnology harnesses the benefits of these symbiotic interactions for agricultural and environmental purposes.

- Thus, mycorrhiza enables plants to all the three things mentioned in the question. Hence, 
d) is the correct answer.
