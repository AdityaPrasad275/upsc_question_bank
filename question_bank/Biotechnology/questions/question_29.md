### Question 29
**3] Other than resistance to pests, what are the prospects for which genetically engineered plants have been created? [2012]**

1. To enable them to withstand drought
2. To increase the nutritive value of the produce
3. To enable them to grow and do photosynthesis in spaceships and space stations
4. To increase their shelf life

Select the correct answer using the codes given below:

a) 1 &amp; 2 Only 
b) 3 &amp; 4 Only

c) 1, 2 &amp; 4 Only 
d) 1, 2, 3 &amp; 4

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1, 2 &amp; 4 Only

**Explanation:**
- Genetically engineered plants, also known as genetically modified (GM) plants or genetically modified organisms (GMOs), are plants that have been modified using genetic engineering techniques to introduce new traits or characteristics that are not naturally occurring in the species through traditional breeding methods.

- Genetic engineering allows scientists to transfer specific genes from one organism to another, regardless of species barriers, enabling the creation of plants with desirable traits such as increased resistance to pests, diseases, or herbicides, improved nutritional content, enhanced tolerance to environmental stresses, or modified growth characteristics.

- Thus, statements 1,2 and 4 reflect the prospects with which GM plants have been created. However, statement 3 i.e. to enable plants to do photosynthesis in spaceships is too far fetched and is not the purpose of current research efforts.
