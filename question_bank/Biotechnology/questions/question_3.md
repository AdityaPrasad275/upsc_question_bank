### Question 3
**2] Consider the following statements: [2024]

Statement I:** India does not import apples from the United States of America.
**Statement-II:** In India, the law prohibits the import of Genetically Modified food without the approval of the competent authority.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

a) Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I

b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct, but Statement-II does not explain Statement-I

c) Statement-I is correct, but Statement-II is incorrect

d) Statement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Statement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct

**Explanation:**
- In the entire 2022-23 season (September-August), India imported 50,000 boxes of apples from the US. This was a drop from the previous annual imports of approximately 5 million boxes before the imposition of the additional duty,” (Reference)

- Further, the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, prohibits import, manufacture, use or sale of GM food in India, without FSSAI’s approval.

- Thus, first statement is incorrect and second is correct, making 
d) as correct answer.
