### Question 30
**1] A genetically engineered form of brinjal, known as the Bt-brinjal, has been developed. The objective of this is? [2011]**


a) To make it pest-resistant.

b) To improve its taste and nutritive qualities.

c) To make it drought-resistant.

d) To make its shelf-life longer.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) To make it pest-resistant.

**Explanation:**
- The main objective of developing Bt brinjal, a genetically modified variety of eggplant, is to provide effective protection against a destructive pest called the fruit and shoot borer (FS
B).

- By incorporating genes from the bacterium Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) into the eggplant’s genome, Bt brinjal produces a protein toxin that selectively targets FSB larvae, thereby reducing crop damage and increasing yields.

- This technology aims to offer farmers an environmentally sustainable and cost-effective solution to manage FSB infestations, minimize pesticide use, and improve the productivity and profitability of brinjal cultivation, contributing to food security and livelihoods in regions where brinjal is a staple crop.

- Thus, 
a) is the correct answer.
