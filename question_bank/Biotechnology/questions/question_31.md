### Question 31
**2] At present, scientists can determine the arrangement or relative positions of genes or DNA sequences on a chromosome. How does this knowledge benefit us? [2011]**

1. It is possible to know the pedigree of livestock.
2. It is possible to understand the causes of all human diseases.
3. It is possible to develop disease-resistant animal breeds.

Which of the statements give above is/are correct?

a) 1 and 2 only 
b) 2 only

c) 1 and 3 only 
d) 1,2, and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- The ability to determine the arrangement or relative positions of genes or DNA sequences on a chromosome, **known as genetic mapping or genome mapping, **has significant implications for various fields, including agriculture and human health.

- By analysing the genetic variations and markers present in the genomes of animals,** researchers can construct pedigrees or family trees that trace the lineage and genetic relationships among animals across generations.**

- In the context of livestock breeding, genetic mapping provides valuable insights into the inheritance patterns of traits and allows for the identification of genetic markers associated with desirable traits, such as disease resistance, productivity, and quality of meat or milk. By analysing the genetic makeup of individual animals and their offspring, breeders can selectively breed animals with desired traits, **leading to the development of disease-resistant animal breeds with improved performance and productivity.**

- **However, with genome mapping, or even any other technology, it is not possible to understand the causes of all human diseases.**

- **Thus, statements 1 and 3 are correct, while 2nd is not.**
