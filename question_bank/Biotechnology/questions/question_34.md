### Question 34
**3] Which of the following types is used by computed tomography employed for visualization of the internal structure of human body? [2007]**


a) X-rays

b) sound Waves

c)Magnetic resonance

d) Radioisotopes

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) X-rays

**Explanation:**
- X-rays are a form of electromagnetic radiation that can penetrate the body and interact with different tissues based on their density and composition.

- In computed tomography (CT), also known as computed axial tomography (CAT), X-ray technology is utilized to create detailed cross-sectional images, or “slices,” of the body’s internal structures.
