### Question 35
**4] Consider the following statements: [2006]**

1. ELISA test is employed as the first and most basic test for an individual to detect cancer.
2. Almost 50% of human beings have Rh+ blood while the remaining have Rh- blood.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Explanation:**
- ELISA (enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay) tests are not typically used as the first and most basic test for detecting cancer. They are more commonly employed in diagnosing infectious diseases, autoimmune disorders, and allergies, as well as in detecting specific proteins or antibodies in biological samples.

- Cancer diagnosis usually involves a combination of methods, including imaging tests, biopsies, blood tests, and molecular assays tailored to the specific type of cancer suspected. Therefore, 1st statement is incorrect.

- Approximately 85% of people worldwide are Rh-positive (Rh+), meaning they have the Rh antigen present on the surface of their red blood cells. The remaining 15% are Rh-negative (Rh-), lacking the Rh antigen. Thus, 2nd statement is also incorrect.
