### Question 36
**5] Consider the following statements: [2004]**

1. Adam Osborne produced the first portable computer.
2. Ian Wilmut created the first cloned sheep.

Which of the statements given above is/ are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- The first portable computer is generally credited to be the Osborne 1, which was produced by Adam Osborne’s company, Osborne Computer Corporation, in 1981.

- Ian Wilmut created the first cloned sheep, named Dolly, in 1996.
