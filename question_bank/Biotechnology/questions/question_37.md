### Question 37
**6] Which of the following pairs is not correctly matched? [2004]**


a) Reinhold Messner: Computer Technology

b) Harlow Shapley: Astronomy

c) Gregor Mendel: Hereditary Theory

d) Godfrey Hounsfield: CT Scan

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Reinhold Messner: Computer Technology

**Explanation:**
- Reinhold Messner is not known for his contributions to computer technology. He is renowned as a mountaineer and explorer, particularly for his solo ascents of the world’s highest peaks without supplemental oxygen. **Therefore, option 
a) is not correctly matched.**

- Harlow Shapley was an American astronomer known for his significant contributions to the field of astronomy. He conducted pioneering research on the size and structure of the Milky Way galaxy and the distribution of globular star clusters. Shapley also played a key role in the “Great Debate” of 1920, where he argued that the Sun was not at the center of the Milky Way but rather located off-center in the galaxy’s spiral arms.

- Gregor Mendel was an Austrian scientist and Augustinian friar who is often referred to as the “father of modern genetics.” He conducted experiments on pea plants in the mid-19th century, meticulously documenting patterns of inheritance for traits such as flower color, seed shape, and plant height. Mendel’s work laid the foundation for the laws of heredity, including the principles of segregation and independent assortment, which form the basis of modern genetics.

- Godfrey Hounsfield was an English electrical engineer who developed the first clinical computed tomography (CT) scanner in the early 1970s. His invention revolutionized medical imaging by providing detailed cross-sectional images of the human body, leading to significant advancements in the diagnosis and treatment of various medical conditions, including brain tumors, vascular diseases, and traumatic injuries. Hounsfield’s pioneering work earned him the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1979.
