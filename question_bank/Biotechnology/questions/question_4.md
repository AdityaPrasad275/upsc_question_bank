### Question 4
**1] ‘Aerial metagenomics’ best refers to which one of the following situations? [2023]**


a) Collecting DNA samples from air in a habitat at one go

b) Understanding the genetic makeup of avian species of a habitat

c) Using air-borne devices to collect blood samples from moving animals

d) Sending drones to inaccessible areas to collect plant and animal samples from land surfaces and water bodies

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Collecting DNA samples from air in a habitat at one go

**Explanation:**
- ‘Aerial metagenomics’ refers to the **study of genetic material collected from the air.** This involves capturing airborne particles, such as microbes, pollen, or spores, and analyzing their DNA to understand the diversity and dynamics of microorganisms present in the atmosphere. This approach helps in monitoring environmental changes, detecting pathogens, and studying the ecology of airborne organisms.
