### Question 40
**9] Canola refers to special type of oil seed mustard varieties bred for human consumption. The main characteristic of these varieties is that the: [2000]**


a) Seeds have very high oil content

b) Oil is rich in unsaturated fatty acids

c) Oil has long shelf-life

d) Oil has very low Erucic acid content

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Oil is rich in unsaturated fatty acids

**Explanation:**
- Canola refers to special types of oilseed mustard varieties that have been bred specifically for human consumption.

- Canola oil has a favourable balance of fatty acids**, including a high content of unsaturated fats and a relatively low level of saturated fats**, contributing to heart health.
