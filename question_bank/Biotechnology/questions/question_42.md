### Question 42
**11] Endoscopy, a technique used to explore the stomach or other inner parts of the body is based on the phenomenon of: [1999]**


a) Total internal reflection

b) Interference

c) Diffractionc

d) Polarisation

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Total internal reflection

**Explanation:**
- Endoscopy is a medical procedure used to examine the interior of a body cavity or organ using a specialized instrument called an endoscope.

- An endoscope is a flexible or rigid tube with a light and camera attached. It can transmit images from inside the body to an external monitor, allowing doctors to see the area being examined.

- Optical fibre is a critical component in endoscopy. **Optical fibres, which work on the principle of total internal reflection,** are used to transmit light from an external light source to the endoscope’s tip. This illuminates the area being examined, providing clear visibility.
