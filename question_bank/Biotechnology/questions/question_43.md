### Question 43
**12] Assertion (
A): Insect resistant transgenic cotton has been produced by inserting Bt gene.: [1999]**

Reason (R): The Bt gene is derived from a bacterium.

a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

b) Both A and R are true, but R is not a correct explanation of A

c) A is true, but R is false 
d) A is false, but R is true

d) A is false, but R is true

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

**Explanation:**
- Bt cotton is a genetically modified variety of cotton that produces an insecticide to combat bollworm pests. The “Bt” stands for Bacillus thuringiensis, a bacterium whose genes are used in the cotton plant to give it pest-resistant properties.

- Thus, option 
a) is correct.
