### Question 44
**13] Assertion (
A): Dolly was the first cloned mammal.: [1999]**

Reason (R): Dolly was produced by in vitro fertilization.

a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A

b) Both A and R are true, but R is not a correct explanation of A

c) A is true, but R is false

d) A is false, but R is true

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) A is false, but R is true

**Explanation:**
- Dolly was not the first ever cloned mammal. That honour belongs to another sheep which was cloned from an embryo cell and born in 1984 in Cambridge, UK. (Reference)

- Dolly the sheep was not produced by in vitro fertilization (IVF). Instead, she was created using a technique called somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT). In SCNT, the nucleus of an adult somatic cell (in this case, a mammary gland cell) is transferred into an unfertilized egg cell whose nucleus has been removed.

- **Thus, both statements are false. An apparent mistake by UPSC. We don’t know if the question was dropped by commission.**
