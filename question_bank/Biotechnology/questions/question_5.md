### Question 5
**2] ‘Microsatellite DNA’ is used in the case of which one of the following? [2023]**


a) Studying the evolutionary relationships among various species of fauna

b) Stimulating ‘stem cells’ to transform into diverse functional tissues

c) Promoting clonal horticultural plants propagation of horticulture plants

d) Assessing the efficacy of drugs by conducting series of drug trials in a population

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Studying the evolutionary relationships among various species of fauna

**Explanation:**
- Microsatellite DNA, also known as simple sequence repeats (SSRs) consists of short, repetitive sequences of DNA, typically 2-6 base pairs long, repeated multiple times in a row. These sequences are scattered throughout the genome and are highly polymorphic, meaning they vary greatly among individuals in a population. This variability makes microsatellites useful for a variety of genetic studies.

- These are used for following purposes:

- Genetic Mapping: Identifying the location of genes and genetic markers.

- Forensic Science: DNA fingerprinting for individual identification.

- Population Genetics: Studying genetic diversity and structure within populations.

- Conservation Biology: Monitoring genetic diversity in endangered species.

- **Evolutionary Biology: Investigating evolutionary relationships and population histories.**
