### Question 6
**1] Consider the following statements: [2022]
**
DNA Barcoding can be a tool to:
1. Assess the age of a plant or animal.
2. Distinguish among species that look alike.
3. Identify undesirable animal or plant materials in processed foods.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 3 only

c) 1 and 2

d) 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- DNA barcoding is a technique used to identify species by analyzing a short, standardized segment of DNA from a specific region of an organism’s genome. This segment, often from the mitochondrial gene cytochrome c oxidase I (COI) in animals, serves as a unique identifier or “barcode” for each species.

- The technique can be used for multiple purposes which include the following:

- **Identify Species: Accurately identify and distinguish between species based on short, standardized regions of DNA.**

- Discover New Species: Detect previously unknown species by comparing DNA sequences to existing databases.

- Monitor Biodiversity: Assess and monitor the biodiversity of ecosystems by identifying the species present in environmental samples.

- **Detect Food Fraud: Verify the authenticity of food products by checking their species composition.**

- Aid in Conservation: Help in conservation efforts by identifying and cataloguing species, particularly in biodiversity hotspots and protected areas.

- Support Forensic Investigations: Assist in wildlife forensics to combat illegal wildlife trade by identifying species from small or degraded samples.
