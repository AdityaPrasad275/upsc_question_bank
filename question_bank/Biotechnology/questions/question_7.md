### Question 7
**2] In the context of vaccines manufactured to prevent COVID-19 pandemic, consider the following statements: [2022]
**
1. The Serum Institute of India produced COVID-19 vaccine named Covishield using mRNA platform.
2. Sputnik V vaccine is manufactured using vector based platform.
3. COVAXIN is an inactivated pathogen ‘based vaccine.

Which of the statements given. above are correct?

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- 1. Covishield is not produced using the mRNA platform. Covishield is a viral vector vaccine developed by AstraZeneca and the University of Oxford and manufactured by the Serum Institute of India.

- 2. Sputnik V is a vector-based vaccine that uses adenovirus vectors to deliver the genetic material coding for the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein.

- 3. COVAXIN, developed by Bharat Biotech in India, is an inactivated vaccine, meaning it uses a killed version of the virus to stimulate an immune response without causing the disease.

- Thus, only 2nd and 3rd statements are correct.
