### Question 9
**1] Bollgard I and Bollgard II technologies are mentioned in the context of: [2021]
**

a) Clonal propagation of crop plants

b) Developing genetically modified crop plants

c) Production of plant growth substances

d) Production of biofertilizers

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Developing genetically modified crop plants

**Explanation:**
- Bollgard I and Bollgard II are genetically modified (GM) cotton technologies developed by Monsanto (now part of Bayer) to provide resistance against certain insect pests, particularly the cotton bollworm.

- Thus, b is correct.
