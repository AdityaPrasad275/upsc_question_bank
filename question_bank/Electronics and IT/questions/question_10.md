### Question
**3] With reference to Web 3.0, consider the following statements: [2022]**

1. Web 3.0 technology enables people to control their own data.
2. In the Web 3.0 world, there can be blockchain based social networks.
3. Web 3.0 is operated by users collectively rather than a corporation.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
a) 1 and 2 only
b) 2 and 3 only
c) 1 and 3 only
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Web 3.0, also known as the “Semantic Web” or “Decentralized Web,” represents the next evolution of the internet, characterized by several key features such as decentralization, blockchain and cryptocurrencies, user control and privacy, interoperability, artificial intelligence (ai) and enhanced user experience.



- **All the statements given above are true in this regard. **



- 1. **Web 3.0 technology enables people to control their own data**: In Web 3.0, users have more control over their personal data compared to Web 2.0, where data is often owned and controlled by corporations. Decentralized technologies like blockchain allow users to own and manage their data directly.



- 2. **In the Web 3.0 world, there can be blockchain-based social networks**: Web 3.0 facilitates the creation of decentralized social networks where user data is stored on a blockchain, offering greater privacy and control over personal information.



- 3. **Web 3.0 is operated by users collectively rather than a corporation**: Web 3.0 promotes decentralization, where power and control are distributed among users rather than centralized corporations. Decentralized governance models allow users to collectively participate in decision-making processes regarding the operation and development of platforms and applications.
