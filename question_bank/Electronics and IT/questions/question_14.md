### Question
**2] With the present state of development, Artificial Intelligence can effectively do which of the following? [2020]**

1. Bring down electricity consumption in industrial units
2. Create meaningful short stories and songs
3. Disease diagnosis
4. Text-to-Speech Conversion
5. Wireless transmission of electrical energy

Select the correct answer using the code given below:
a) 1, 2, 3 &amp; 5 only
b) 1, 3 &amp; 4 only
c) 2, 4 &amp; 5 only
d) 1, 2, 3, 4 &amp; 5

**Answer:**
Ans: b) 1, 3 &amp; 4 only

**Explanation:**
- AI, or Artificial Intelligence, is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. This includes learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding. The examples of these tools include ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Amazon’s product recommendation system, Tesla’s Autopilot system etc.



- Things constantly change in the field of science and tech. This question was asked in 2020, **at that time and even now,** the option 5 i.e. wireless transmission of electrical energy is not possible using AI.



- This fact, then eliminates option a, c and d, leaving with only option b as answer, which is also the official answer.



- Today, the facts are different. Artificial intelligence can do the tasks 1-4, and the correct answer would be 1,2,3 and 4 only (which is not there in the options).
