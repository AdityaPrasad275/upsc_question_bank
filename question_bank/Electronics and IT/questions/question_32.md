### Question
**1] In addition to fingerprint scanning, which of the following can be used in the biometric identification of a person? [2014]**

1. Iris scanning
2. Retinal scanning
3. Voice recognition

Select the correct answer using the code given below.
a) 1 only
b) 2 and 3 only
c) 1 and 3 only
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- All the biometric identification methods given in the options are unique to each individual due to specific physiological and behavioural characteristics.



- The **iris**, which is the coloured part of the eye surrounding the pupil, has a complex and unique pattern for each person, even among identical twins. An iris scanner captures an image of the iris and uses algorithms to analyse its unique patterns, such as rings, furrows, and freckles, for identification.



- The **retina**, located at the back of the eye, has a unique pattern of blood vessels that is different for each person. retinal scanner uses a low-intensity light to scan the pattern of blood vessels in the retina. This pattern is then compared to stored templates for identification.



- Each person’s **voice** has unique characteristics based on the shape and size of their vocal cords, mouth, and nasal passages, as well as their speech patterns. Voice recognition systems analyse various features of a person’s voice, such as pitch, tone, and rhythm, to create a unique voice print. This voice print is used for identifying and verifying individuals.



- **Thus, all the methods are correct and d is the right answer.**
