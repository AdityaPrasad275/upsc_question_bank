### Question
**1] Which among the following do / does not belong / belongs to the GSM family of wireless technologies? [2010]**

a) EDGE&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
b) LTE
c) DSL
d) Both EDGE and LTE

**Answer:**
Ans: c) DSL

**Explanation:**
- The GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) family of wireless technologies is a standard developed to describe protocols cellular networks. The various technologies in GSM are:



- GSM (2G): Digital voice calls, SMS, basic data services.



- GPRS (General Packet Radio Service): Moderate-speed data transfer up to 114 Kbps.



- EDGE (Enhanced Data rates for GSM Evolution): Faster data transfer up to 384 Kbps.



- UMTS (Universal Mobile Telecommunications System): 3G technology with speeds up to 2 Mbps.



- HSPA (High-Speed Packet Access): Includes HSDPA (up to 14.4 Mbps) and HSUPA (up to 5.76 Mbps).



- HSPA+ (Evolved High-Speed Packet Access): Advanced 3G with speeds up to 42 Mbps.



- LTE (Long-Term Evolution): 4G technology with speeds up to 300 Mbps.



- LTE-A (LTE Advanced): Enhanced 4G with speeds up to 1 Gbps.



- **DSL (Digital Subscriber Line) is a technology that provides high-speed internet connections over traditional telephone lines.**



- **Hence c) DSL is answer.**
