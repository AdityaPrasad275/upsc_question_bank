### Question
**2] Consider the following: [2010]**

1. Bluetooth device
2. Cordless phone
3. Microwave oven
4. Wi – Fi device

Which of the above can operate between 2.4 and 2.5 GHz range of radio frequency band?
a) 1 and 2 only&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) 3 and 4 only
c) 1, 2 and 4 only&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
- The frequency ranges of these technologies are:



- **Bluetooth device:** 2.4 GHz (specifically, 2.400 to 2.485 GHz).



- **Cordless phone:** Typically, 900 MHz, 1.9 GHz (DECT), 2.4 GHz, or 5.8 GHz, depending on the model and technology.



- **Microwave oven:** 2.45 GHz.



- **Wi-Fi device:** 2.4 GHz (2.400 to 2.4835 GHz) and 5 GHz (5.150 to 5.825 GHz, with specific sub-ranges depending on the country and regulations). A new 6 GHz band was also introduced with Wi-Fi 6E



- **Thus, option d is correct**
