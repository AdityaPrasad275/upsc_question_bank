### Question
**3] WIMaX is related to which one of the following? [2009]**

a) Biotechnology
b) Space technology
c) Missile technology
d) Communication technology

**Answer:**
Ans: d) Communication technology

**Explanation:**
- WiMAX (Worldwide Interoperability for Microwave Access) is a wireless communication standard based on the IEEE 802.16 family, designed to provide high-speed internet access over long distances.



- Operating in several frequency ranges, typically 2.3 GHz, 2.5 GHz, and 3.5 GHz, WiMAX can cover distances up to 30 miles (about 50 kilometers) for fixed stations and 3-10 miles (about 5-15 kilometers) for mobile stations.



- It offers high data rates, theoretically up to 1 Gbps for fixed stations and 100 Mbps for mobile stations, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.



- WiMAX was developed to offer wireless broadband services as an alternative to wired broadband technologies like DSL and cable, enabling more flexible and widespread internet connectivity.



- **Hence option d) is correct answer.**
