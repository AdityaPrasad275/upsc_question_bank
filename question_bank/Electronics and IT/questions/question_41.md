### Question
**7] The memory of a computer is commonly expressed in terms of Kilobytes or Megabytes. A byte is made up of: [2000]**

a) eight binary digits&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
b) eight decimal digits
c) two binary digits
d) two decimal digits

**Answer:**
Ans: a) eight binary digits&nbsp;&nbsp;

**Explanation:**
- A byte is made up of a group of binary digits, commonly referred to as bits. **Specifically, a byte consists of 8 bits. **Each bit can have a value of either 0 or 1, representing the smallest unit of data in computing. By combining 8 bits into a byte, computers can represent a wider range of values and characters, allowing for the storage and processing of text, numbers, and other types of data. Bytes are the fundamental building blocks of digital information storage and processing in computers.
