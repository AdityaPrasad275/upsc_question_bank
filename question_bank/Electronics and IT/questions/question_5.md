### Question
**1] Consider the following activities: [2024]

**1. Identification of narcotics on passengers at airports or in aircraft
2. Monitoring of precipitation
3. Tracking the migration of animals

In how many of the above activities can the radars be used?
a) Only one
b) Only two
c) All three
d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: c) All three

**Explanation:**
- A radar system (Radio Detection and Ranging) uses radio waves to detect and locate objects. It sends out electromagnetic waves, which bounce off objects and return to the radar’s receiver. By analyzing these returning signals, the radar system calculates the object’s distance, speed, and sometimes shape or direction.



- **Radars are extensively used in weather monitoring to track precipitation patterns**, such as rain, snow, and storms.



- **Some radars can track animal migrations**, particularly bird flocks, which helps in understanding their movement patterns.



- The use of radar for the identification of narcotics on passengers is not known. However, we did find some rare evidence suggesting it’s possible. Please refer this article for more details.
