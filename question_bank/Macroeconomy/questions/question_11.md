### Question
**2] If a commodity is provided free to the public by Government, then: [2018]

**a) The opportunity cost is zero.
b) The opportunity cost is ignored.
c) The opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the tax paying public.
d) The opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the Government.

**Answer:**
Ans: c) The opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the tax paying public.

**Explanation:**
When the government provides a commodity for free to the public, it doesn’t mean there’s no opportunity cost. The opportunity cost refers to the value of the next best alternative foregone when a choice is made. In this scenario:





c) The opportunity cost is transferred from the consumers of the product to the tax-paying public.





When the government provides a commodity for free, it is typically funded through taxes. Taxpayers bear the opportunity cost because the resources used to produce and provide the free commodity could have been allocated elsewhere, potentially to provide other goods or services or to reduce taxes. Therefore, taxpayers bear the opportunity cost of the free commodity.
