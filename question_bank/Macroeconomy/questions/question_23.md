### Question
**3] A ‘closed economy” is an economy in which? [2011]**

a) The money supply is fully controlled.
b) Deficit financing&nbsp; takes place.
c) Only exports take place.
d) Neither exports nor imports take place.

**Answer:**
Ans:  d) Neither exports nor imports take place.

**Explanation:**
- **Closed economy:**&nbsp;A closed economy is a theoretical concept that refers to an economy that has no interaction with other economies. It’s a simplified model and doesn’t exist perfectly in the real world. However, it helps us understand the basic functioning of an economy without external influences.



- **Key feature:**&nbsp;The defining characteristic of a closed economy is the absence of international trade. This means there are&nbsp;**neither exports nor imports**&nbsp;of goods, services, or capital. The economy relies solely on its own domestic production and consumption to meet its needs.
