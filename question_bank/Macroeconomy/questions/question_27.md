### Question
**4] Which one of the following statements is an appropriate description of deflation? [2010]**

a) It is a sudden fall in the value of a currency against other currencies.
b) It is a persistent recession in both the financial and real sectors of economy.
c) It is a persistent fall in the general price level of goods and services.
d) It is a fall in the rate of inflation over a period of time.

**Answer:**
Ans:  c) It is a persistent fall in the general price level of goods and services.

**Explanation:**
Here’s why the other options are incorrect:






- **a) Sudden fall in the value of a currency:**&nbsp;This describes depreciation, which is a decline in the value of a currency relative to other currencies. Deflation refers to a decrease in the general price level of goods and services within a country, not necessarily affecting the exchange rate with other currencies.



- **b) Persistent recession in both financial and real sectors:**&nbsp;A recession is a period of negative economic growth, but it doesn’t necessarily involve deflation. While deflation can worsen a recession, they are not the same concept.



- **d) Fall in the rate of inflation over a period of time:**&nbsp;Disinflation is a slowdown in the rate of inflation, but it doesn’t necessarily mean prices are falling. Deflation requires a sustained decrease in the general price level, not just a slower increase.




Deflation can have both positive and negative consequences:






- **Positive:**&nbsp;It can lead to increased purchasing power for consumers as their money can buy more goods and services.



- **Negative:**&nbsp;It can discourage spending and investment as people wait for prices to fall further. This can hinder economic growth.
