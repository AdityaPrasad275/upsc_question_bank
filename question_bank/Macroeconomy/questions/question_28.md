### Question
**5] In the context of independent India’s Economy, which one of the following was the earliest event to take place? [2009]**

a) Nationalisation of Insurance companies
b) Nationalisation of State Bank of India
c) Enactment of Banking Regulation act
d) Introduction of First Five-Year Plan

**Answer:**
Ans:  c) Enactment of Banking Regulation act

**Explanation:**
Here’s why:






- **Banking Regulation Act:**&nbsp;This act was passed in 1949. It aimed to regulate banking operations in India and ensure the safety and security of depositors’ money.



- **Nationalisation of State Bank of India (SBI):**&nbsp;This occurred in 1955, several years after the Banking Regulation Act.



- **Nationalisation of Insurance Companies:**&nbsp;Life insurance companies were nationalized in 1956, following the SBI nationalization.



- **Introduction of First Five-Year Plan:**&nbsp;The planning commission was set up in 1950 and the First Five Year Plan was launched in 1951.




Therefore, the enactment of the Banking Regulation Act in 1949 predates all the other listed events.
