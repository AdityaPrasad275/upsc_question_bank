### Question
**3] Consider the following statements: [2024]

Statement I: **If the United States of America (USA) were to default on its debt, holders of US Treasury Bonds will not be able to exercise their claims to receive payment.
**Statement-II:** The USA Government debt is not backed by any hard assets, but only by the faith of the Government.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?
a) Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I
b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct, but Statement-II does not explain Statement-I
c) Statement-I is correct, but Statement-II is incorrect
d) Statement-I is incorrect, but Statement-II is correct

**Answer:**
Ans: a) Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II explains Statement-I

**Explanation:**
- Sovereign debt refers to the money borrowed by a national government from domestic or international lenders to finance its expenditures, such as infrastructure projects, public services, or debt refinancing. It is typically issued in the form of bonds and can be owed to foreign governments, international organizations, or private investors.



- A sovereign default is a nation’s failure to repay its debt obligations.



- Sovereign debts are backed primarily by the creditworthiness and economic stability of the issuing country. Unlike corporate or individual debt**, sovereign debt often lacks physical collateral and depends on the issuing country’s willingness and ability to repay.**



- Based on above information, both the given sentences are correct and a) is correct option.
