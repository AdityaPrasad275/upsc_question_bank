### Question
**8] Which reference to the Indian economy, consider the following activities: [2002]**

1. Agriculture, Forestry and Fishing
2. Manufacturing
3. Trade, Hotels Transport and Communication
4. Financing, Insurance, Real Estate and Business services

The decreasing order of the contribution of these sectors to the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) at factor cost at constant prices (2000’01) is
a) 3, 1, 2, 4&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) 1, 3, 4, 2
c) 3, 4, 1, 2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) 1, 3, 2, 4

**Answer:**
Ans:  c) 3, 4, 1, 2

**Explanation:**
- **Trade, Hotels Transport and Communication:** This sector, often referred to as the tertiary sector, has become the dominant contributor to India’s GDP in recent years. It encompasses a wide range of activities like trade, tourism, transportation, and communication services.



- **Financing, Insurance, Real Estate and Business Services:** This sector, also part of the tertiary sector, has grown significantly and contributes a substantial share to GDP. It includes activities like banking, insurance, real estate, and professional services.



- **Agriculture, Forestry and Fishing:** While agriculture remains an important sector employing a large portion of the workforce, its contribution to GDP has been declining over time. This is a common trend in developing economies as they transition towards a more service-oriented economy.



- **Manufacturing:** The industrial sector plays a significant role, encompassing manufacturing, construction, and utilities. While its contribution is lower than the top two sectors, it’s still crucial for economic growth and development.
