### Question
**17] In an open economy, the national income (Y) of the economy is: (C, I, G, X, M stand for Consumption, Investment, Govt. Expenditure, total exports and total imports respectively: [2000]**

a) Y = C + I + G + X
b) Y = C + I + G – X + M
c) Y = C + I + G + (X – M)
d) Y = C + I – G + X – M

**Answer:**
Ans:  c) Y = C + I + G + (X – M)

**Explanation:**
- **National Income (Y):**&nbsp;This refers to the total income earned by the residents of a country within a given period (usually a year).



- **C (Consumption):**&nbsp;The value of final goods and services consumed by households in the economy.



- **I (Investment):**&nbsp;The value of new capital goods produced in the economy.



- **G (Government Expenditure):**&nbsp;The value of final goods and services purchased by the government.




**Formula Explanation:**





The national income in an open economy considers domestic production (C + I + G) and the net effect of international trade (X – M).






- **Domestic Production (C + I + G):**&nbsp;This captures the value of goods and services produced within the country, regardless of who consumes them (domestic households or foreign buyers).



- **Net Effect of Trade (X – M):**&nbsp;Exports generate income for the country, while imports represent income flowing out. The difference (X – M) captures the net impact of trade on national income.




Therefore, Y = C + I + G + (X – M) accurately represents the national income in an open economy.
