### Question
**20] The supply-side economics lays greater emphasis on the point of view of: [1998]**

a) Producer&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) Global economy
c) Consumer&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) Middle-man

**Answer:**
Ans:  a) Producer

**Explanation:**
- **Focus on Production:**&nbsp;Supply-side economics argues that economic growth can be most effectively stimulated by increasing the aggregate supply of goods and services within an economy.



- **Producer Incentives:**&nbsp;This theory emphasizes policies that incentivize businesses and producers to increase their output. These incentives can take various forms, such as tax cuts, deregulation, and a stable business environment.



- **Lower Prices (Eventually):**&nbsp;In theory, with increased supply and more competition, prices may eventually fall, benefiting consumers in the long run. However, this is not guaranteed.
