**1] ****Consider the following statements:** **[2025/A-49]

**I. No virus can survive in ocean waters.
II. No virus can infect bacteria.
III. No virus can change the cellular transcriptional activity in host cells.
Which of the statements given above are correct?


a) Only one

b) Only two

c) All the three

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) None

**Explanation:**
- Viruses can survive in oceans, infect bacteria, and alter host cell transcription. Thus, **all the given statements are incorrect.**
