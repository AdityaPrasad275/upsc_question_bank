### Question 13
**1] Which one of the following is the process involved in photosynthesis? [2014]**


a) Potential energy is released to form free energy.

b) Free energy is converted into potential energy and stored.

c) Food is oxidized to release carbon dioxide and water.

d) Oxygen is taken, and carbon dioxide and water vapour are given out.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Free energy is converted into potential energy and stored.

**Explanation:**
Photosynthesis is a process by which plants capture light energy and convert it into chemical energy stored in the form of glucose (a sugar). Here’s a breakdown of the options:

- **
a) Incorrect:** Photosynthesis uses light energy (a form of kinetic energy) to create chemical bonds in glucose (potential energy). It doesn’t involve releasing free energy.

- **
b) Correct:** Photosynthesis utilizes light energy (free energy) to power the conversion of carbon dioxide and water into glucose (potential energy). This stored energy can be used by the plant for various functions.

- **
c) Incorrect:** This describes cellular respiration, the opposite process of photosynthesis. Cellular respiration breaks down glucose to release energy for the cell’s activities.

- **
d) Incorrect:** While plants do take in oxygen during photosynthesis, it’s not the primary purpose. The main function is to capture light energy and convert it into usable chemical energy (glucose). The oxygen released is a byproduct of the process.

So, photosynthesis involves converting free energy (light) into potential energy (glucose) for the plant.
