### Question 14
**2] Among the following organisms, which one does not belong to the class of other three? [2014]
**

a) Crab

b) Mite

c) Scorpion

d) Spider

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Crab

**Explanation:**
Picking the odd one out: Crab!

This question tests your knowledge of animal classification. Here’s why Crab is the outsider:

- **Arachnid buddies:** Mites, scorpions, and spiders are close relatives. They all belong to the class Arachnida within the animal phylum Arthropoda. Think eight legs, pincers for grasping food, and a body in two sections (fused head-thorax and abdomen).

**Crab’s different class:** Crabs are in the Crustacea class, also under Arthropoda. Key differences separate them from arachnids:

- Legged advantage: Crabs have ten legs, while arachnids have eight.

- Feelers: Crabs sport two pairs of antennae, missing on arachnids.

- Breathing styles: Crabs use gills for underwater breathing, unlike arachnids with book lungs or air tubes.
