### Question 18
**3] Consider the following minerals: [2013]**

1. Calcium
2. Iron
3. Sodium

Which of the minerals given above is/are required by human body for the contraction of muscles?

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
Required Minerals for Muscle Contraction:

Muscle contraction is a complex process that involves the interaction of various minerals and nutrients. The following minerals are required by the human body for muscle contraction:

1. Calcium: Calcium plays a vital role in muscle contraction. It is required for the release of neurotransmitters, which stimulate the muscle cells to contract. Calcium is also essential for maintaining the integrity of the muscle cells and for regulating the contraction and relaxation of the muscle fibers.

2. Iron: Iron is required for the production of hemoglobin, which is a protein that carries oxygen to the muscle cells. During muscle contraction, the muscle cells require a constant supply of oxygen to produce energy. Iron also plays a crucial role in the metabolism of energy, which is necessary for muscle contraction.

3. Sodium: Sodium is an electrolyte that is required for the transmission of nerve impulses to the muscle cells. It is also necessary for maintaining the balance of fluids in the body, which is crucial for muscle function. Therefore, all the given minerals, i.e. calcium, iron, and sodium, are required by the human body for muscle contraction.
