### Question 2
**1] Which one of the following is synthesised in human body that dilates blood vessels and increases blood flow? [2024]

**
a) Nitric oxide

b) Nitrous oxide

c) Nitrogen dioxide

d) Nitrogen pentoxide

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Nitric oxide

**Explanation:**
- **Nitric oxide (NO**) is synthesized in the human body by the enzyme nitric oxide synthase (NOS)

- Nitric oxide (NO) regulates vascular tone and blood flow by activating soluble guanylate cyclase (sG
C) in the vascular smooth muscle.

- Thus, 
a) Nitric oxide is correct answer.
