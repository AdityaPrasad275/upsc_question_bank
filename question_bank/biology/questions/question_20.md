### Question 20
**5] Many transplanted seedlings do not grow because? [2013]**


a) The new soil does not contain favourable minerals

b) Most of the root hairs grip the new soil too hard

c) Most of the root hairs are lost during transplantation

d) Leaves get damaged during transplantation

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Most of the root hairs are lost during transplantation

**Explanation:**
The option that best explains why many transplanted seedlings do not grow is: Most of the root hairs are lost during transplantation.

During the process of transplanting seedlings, the root system is often disturbed, leading to the loss of many root hairs. Root hairs are responsible for absorbing water and nutrients from the soil. When a significant number of root hairs are lost, the ability of the seedling to absorb essential resources is compromised. As a result, the transplanted seedlings may struggle to establish a strong root system and may not grow properly.
