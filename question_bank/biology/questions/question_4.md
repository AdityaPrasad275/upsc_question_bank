### Question 4
**1] Which one of the following statements best describes the role of B cells and T cells in the human body? [2022]
**

a) They protect the body from environmental allergens.

b) They alleviate the body’s pain and inflammation

c) They act as immunosuppressants in the body.

d) They protect the body from diseases caused by pathogens.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) They protect the body from diseases caused by pathogens.

**Explanation:**
B cells and T cells are fundamental components of the adaptive immune system, collectively responsible for defending the body against diseases caused by pathogens. B cells are primarily involved in humoral immunity, producing antibodies that recognize and neutralize specific antigens present on pathogens. These antibodies mark the pathogens for destruction by other immune cells or complement proteins. T cells, on the other hand, are crucial for cell-mediated immunity. They directly identify and eliminate infected cells by recognizing specific antigens presented on the surface of these cells.

Through their complementary functions, B cells and T cells orchestrate targeted and coordinated immune responses tailored to combat specific pathogens encountered by the body. This dynamic interplay between B cells and T cells ensures effective immune surveillance, memory formation, and long-term protection against infectious agents.

In summary, B cells and T cells play pivotal roles in safeguarding the body from diseases caused by pathogens by mounting specialized immune responses tailored to recognize and eliminate specific threats.
