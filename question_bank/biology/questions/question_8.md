### Question 8
**3] Consider the following statements: [2021]
**
1) Adenoviruses have single-stranded DNA genomes whereas retroviruses have double-stranded DNA genomes.
2) Common cold is sometime caused by an adenovirus whereas AIDS is caused by a retrovirus.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only

b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2

d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 only

**Explanation:**
**Statement 1: **Adenoviruses have single-stranded DNA genomes whereas retroviruses have double-stranded DNA genomes.
Adenoviruses and retroviruses are two different types of viruses that have distinct characteristics, including the structure of their genomes.

**Adenoviruses:**
– Adenoviruses are a group of viruses that have double-stranded DNA genomes, not single-stranded DNA genomes as mentioned in statement 1.
– The DNA genome of adenoviruses is linear and non-segmented, meaning it is not divided into smaller pieces.
– Adenoviruses can cause a variety of illnesses in humans, including respiratory infections, conjunctivitis, and gastroenteritis.

**Retroviruses:**
– Retroviruses, on the other hand, have single-stranded RNA genomes, not double-stranded DNA genomes as mentioned in statement 1.
– However, retroviruses have a unique characteristic called reverse transcription, where their RNA genome is converted into DNA within the host cell.
– This DNA is then integrated into the host cell’s genome and can be transcribed and translated to produce viral proteins.
– Retroviruses are responsible for several important human diseases, including AIDS (Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome), caused by the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV).

**Conclusion:** Based on the information provided, statement 1 is incorrect. Adenoviruses have double-stranded DNA genomes, while retroviruses have single-stranded RNA genomes that are reverse transcribed into DNA.

**Statement 2:** Common cold is sometimes caused by an adenovirus whereas AIDS is caused by a retrovirus.
The common cold and AIDS are two distinct diseases caused by different types of viruses.

**Common Cold:**
– The common cold is a viral infection that predominantly affects the upper respiratory tract.
– While there are several viruses that can cause the common cold, adenoviruses are one of the many possible culprits.
– Adenoviruses can cause symptoms such as runny nose, sore throat, cough, and mild fever, among others.

**AIDS (Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome):**
– AIDS is a chronic, life-threatening condition caused by the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), which is a retrovirus.
– HIV attacks the immune system, specifically the CD4+ T cells, and weakens the body’s ability to fight off infections and diseases.
– If left untreated, HIV can progress to AIDS, which is characterized by severe immunodeficiency and the increased susceptibility to opportunistic infections and certain types of cancer.

**Conclusion: **Based on the information provided, statement 2 is correct. The common cold can be caused by adenoviruses, while AIDS is caused by the retrovirus HIV.
