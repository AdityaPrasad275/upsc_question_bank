### Question 10
**1] Fruits stored in cold chamber exhibit long storage life because ___: [2013]
**

a) Exposure to sunlight is prevented

b) Concentration of Carbon dioxide in the environment is increased

c) Rate of respiration is decreased

d) There is an increase in humidity

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Rate of respiration is decreased

**Explanation:**
Storing fruits in a cold chamber extends their storage life primarily by reducing the rate of respiration. Respiration is a vital metabolic process in fruits, wherein they consume oxygen and release carbon dioxide, water, and energy. Lowering the temperature slows down biochemical reactions, including respiration, which significantly reduces the fruits’ metabolic activity. As a result, the fruits undergo less physiological changes associated with ripening, such as softening, color changes, and flavor development.

Cold temperatures also inhibit the growth of microorganisms that can cause spoilage, further preserving the fruits’ quality. Additionally, cold storage helps to minimize water loss from fruits, preventing dehydration and maintaining their firmness and juiciness.

Moreover, storing fruits in a cold chamber creates a controlled environment where exposure to sunlight and fluctuating temperatures is minimized. This controlled environment helps to maintain optimal conditions for fruit storage, ensuring that the fruits remain fresh for a longer period.

In essence, by slowing down respiration, inhibiting microbial growth, reducing water loss, and providing a controlled environment, cold storage significantly extends the storage life of fruits, allowing them to be stored for extended periods without compromising their quality or freshness.
