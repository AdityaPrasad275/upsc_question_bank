### Question 4
**1] Bisphenol A (BP
A), a cause of concern, is a structural/key component in the manufacture of which of the following kinds of plastics? [2021]
**

a) Low-density polyethylene

b) Polycarbonate

c) Polyethylene terephthalate

d) Polyvinyl chloride

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Polycarbonate

**Explanation:**
Bisphenol A (BP
A) is a chemical compound utilized as a key component in the production of polycarbonate plastics. Polycarbonate plastics are valued for their durability, clarity, and heat resistance, making them ideal for a wide range of applications, including food and beverage containers, eyeglass lenses, medical devices, and electronic equipment. However, concerns have arisen regarding the potential health effects of BPA exposure, particularly its ability to mimic estrogen and potentially disrupt hormone levels in humans. Research has linked BPA exposure to various adverse health outcomes, including reproductive disorders, developmental abnormalities, and increased risk of certain cancers. Due to these concerns, there has been a shift towards BPA-free alternatives in various consumer products, and regulatory agencies worldwide have imposed restrictions on the use of BPA in certain applications. Thus, while polycarbonate plastics offer valuable properties, the presence of BPA has prompted scrutiny and efforts to find safer alternatives.
