### Question 5
**2] Water can dissolve more substances than any other liquid because: [2021]
**

a) It is dipolar in nature

b) It is a good conductor of heat

c) It has high value of specific heat

d) It is an oxide of hydrogen

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) It is dipolar in nature

**Explanation:**
Water’s remarkable ability to dissolve a wide variety of substances stems from its unique molecular structure and polarity. A water molecule consists of two hydrogen atoms bonded to one oxygen atom, forming a bent shape. The oxygen atom, being more electronegative, exerts a stronger pull on the shared electrons, creating a partial negative charge (δ-) on the oxygen and partial positive charges (δ+) on the hydrogen atoms. This polarity gives water a dipole moment, allowing it to interact with other polar molecules or ions through electrostatic attractions.

Furthermore, water molecules can form hydrogen bonds with each other and with other molecules. These hydrogen bonds enable water to effectively surround and solvate solute particles, breaking them apart and dispersing them throughout the solvent.

This combination of polarity and hydrogen bonding makes water an exceptionally effective solvent, capable of dissolving a vast range of substances, including salts, sugars, acids, and gases. This property is fundamental to many biological processes, as water serves as the medium in which biochemical reactions occur within living organisms. Thus, water’s role as the “universal solvent” is essential for life as we know it.
