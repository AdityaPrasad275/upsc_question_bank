**1] ‘Wolbachia method’ is sometimes talked about with reference to which one of the following? [2023]**


a) Controlling the viral diseases spread by mosquitoes

b) Converting crop residues into packing material

c) Producing biodegradable plastics

d) Producing biochar from thermo- chemical conversion of biomass.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Controlling the viral diseases spread by mosquitoes

**Explanation:**
The Wolbachia method is a groundbreaking approach to controlling viral diseases spread by mosquitoes, particularly diseases like dengue fever, Zika virus, and chikungunya virus. Wolbachia is a genus of bacteria that naturally infects many insects, including mosquitoes. When certain strains of Wolbachia are introduced into mosquito populations, they interfere with the mosquitoes’ ability to transmit these diseases to humans.

The mechanism behind the Wolbachia method is multifaceted. Firstly, Wolbachia-infected mosquitoes exhibit a reduced ability to harbor and transmit viruses. Secondly, Wolbachia can interfere with the replication of viruses within the mosquito, further limiting their ability to spread. Additionally, when Wolbachia-infected mosquitoes mate with wild mosquitoes, the resulting offspring have a reduced ability to transmit diseases, leading to a gradual decline in disease transmission within the mosquito population.

This method offers a promising alternative to traditional mosquito control measures, such as insecticides, which can have environmental and health concerns. Moreover, it has the potential to be a sustainable, long-term solution to mosquito-borne diseases, as Wolbachia-infected mosquitoes can persist in the environment and continue to reduce disease transmission over time. The Wolbachia method represents a significant advancement in public health strategies, offering hope for the effective control of mosquito-borne viral diseases worldwide.
