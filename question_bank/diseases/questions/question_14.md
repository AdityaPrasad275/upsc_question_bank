### Question 14
**7] “Athlete’s Foot” is a disease caused by: [2001]**


a) Bacteria 
b) Fungus

c) Protozoan 
d) Nematode

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Fungus

**Explanation:**
Athlete’s foot, medically known as tinea pedis, is a common fungal infection that affects the skin on the feet. It is caused by fungi, specifically dermatophytes, which thrive in warm, moist environments like shoes and locker rooms. These fungi can infect the outer layers of the skin, leading to symptoms such as itching, burning, redness, cracking, and peeling of the skin between the toes or on the soles of the feet.

Fungal infections like athlete’s foot are highly contagious and can spread through direct contact with infected skin or contact with contaminated surfaces. Factors such as wearing tight or poorly ventilated shoes, sweaty feet, and walking barefoot in public places increase the risk of contracting the infection.

Treatment typically involves antifungal medications, either topical creams, powders, or oral medications, to eliminate the fungus and relieve symptoms. Additionally, practicing good foot hygiene, keeping the feet clean and dry, wearing breathable footwear, and avoiding sharing personal items like socks and shoes can help prevent athlete’s foot and reduce the risk of recurrence.
