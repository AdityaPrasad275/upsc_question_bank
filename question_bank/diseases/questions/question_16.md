### Question 16
**9] Lathyrism is caused by excessive consumption of: [1999]**


a) Kesari dal 
b) Mustard oil

c) Polished rice 
d) Mushrooms

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Kesari dal

**Explanation:**
Lathyrism is a neurological disorder caused by the excessive consumption of kesari dal, also known as khesari dal (option 
a). Kesari dal is a type of legume commonly grown in parts of India and Africa. It contains a toxin called beta-N-oxalyl-L-alpha-beta-diaminopropionic acid (ODAP), also known as β-ODAP, which is responsible for the development of lathyrism.

Excessive consumption of kesari dal over a prolonged period can lead to the accumulation of β-ODAP in the body. This toxin affects the nervous system, particularly the spinal cord, leading to symptoms such as weakness and stiffness in the lower limbs, difficulty in walking, and muscle wasting. In severe cases, lathyrism can result in paralysis of the lower limbs.

Preventing lathyrism involves reducing the consumption of kesari dal or using appropriate processing methods to detoxify the dal before consumption. Boiling, soaking, and fermentation are some methods used to reduce the levels of β-ODAP in kesari dal, making it safer for consumption while still retaining its nutritional value. Additionally, promoting dietary diversity and awareness about the risks associated with excessive kesari dal consumption are important preventive measures.
