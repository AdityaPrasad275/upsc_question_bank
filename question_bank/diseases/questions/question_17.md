**Answer:**
Ans:

**Explanation:**
- I. Malaria – 
D) Protozoan: Malaria is caused by protozoan parasites of the genus Plasmodium, with Plasmodium falciparum, Plasmodium vivax, Plasmodium malariae, and Plasmodium ovale being the main species that infect humans. These protozoa are transmitted through the bite of infected Anopheles mosquitoes.

- II. Poliomyelitis – 
C) Virus: Poliomyelitis, commonly known as polio, is caused by the poliovirus, which is a type of RNA virus belonging to the Picornaviridae family. Poliovirus primarily infects the gastrointestinal tract but can invade the nervous system, leading to paralysis in severe cases.

- III. Tuberculosis – 
B) Bacteria: Tuberculosis is caused by the bacterium Mycobacterium tuberculosis. This bacterium primarily affects the lungs but can also infect other parts of the body. Tuberculosis is highly contagious and spreads through the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes.

- IV. Ringworm – 
A) Fungi: Ringworm, also known as dermatophytosis, is a fungal infection of the skin or scalp. It is caused by various species of dermatophyte fungi, including Trichophyton, Microsporum, and Epidermophyton. Despite its name, ringworm is not caused by a worm but rather by these fungi, which thrive on the outer layer of the skin, causing characteristic ring-shaped rashes.
