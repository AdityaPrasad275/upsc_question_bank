### Question 21
**14] People drinking water from a shallow hand pump are likely to suffer from all of the following diseases except: [1996]**


a) Cholera 
b) Typhoid

c) Jaundice 
d) Fluorosis

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) FluorosisII

**Explanation:**
Drinking water from a shallow hand pump poses risks of several waterborne diseases, but fluorosis is not among them. Fluorosis is caused by the excessive intake of fluoride, typically from drinking water with high fluoride levels. It leads to dental and skeletal problems due to the accumulation of fluoride in bones and teeth over time.

However, diseases like cholera, typhoid, and jaundice are commonly associated with contaminated water sources like shallow hand pumps. Cholera is caused by the bacterium Vibrio cholerae, often found in contaminated water sources, leading to severe diarrhea and dehydration. Typhoid fever, caused by Salmonella typhi, spreads through contaminated water or food, resulting in high fever, abdominal pain, and gastrointestinal symptoms. Jaundice, caused by hepatitis viruses (such as hepatitis A and E), can be transmitted through contaminated water sources, leading to yellowing of the skin and eyes, fever, and abdominal pain.

Thus, while drinking water from a shallow hand pump can pose risks of cholera, typhoid, and jaundice, it does not lead to fluorosis.
