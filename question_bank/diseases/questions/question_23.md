### Question 23
**16] Which one of the following antimicrobial drugs is suitable for treatment of both tuberculosis and leprosy? [1995]**


a) Isoniazid

b) p-aminosalicylic acid

c) Streptomycin

d) Rifampicin

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Rifampicin

**Explanation:**
Rifampicin is a versatile antimicrobial drug that is effective against both tuberculosis (T
B) and leprosy. In tuberculosis treatment, rifampicin is a cornerstone medication, forming part of the standard multidrug therapy regimen due to its potent bactericidal activity against Mycobacterium tuberculosis, the bacterium responsible for TB. It works by inhibiting bacterial RNA synthesis, thereby disrupting bacterial replication and growth.

Similarly, rifampicin is a crucial component of the multidrug therapy regimen for leprosy, along with other antibiotics such as dapsone and clofazimine. Rifampicin demonstrates activity against Mycobacterium leprae, the bacterium that causes leprosy, contributing to the effective management and cure of the disease.

The dual effectiveness of rifampicin against both TB and leprosy makes it a valuable tool in combating these infectious diseases. Its inclusion in treatment regimens for both diseases highlights its broad spectrum of antimicrobial activity and its importance in global efforts to control and eradicate tuberculosis and leprosy.
