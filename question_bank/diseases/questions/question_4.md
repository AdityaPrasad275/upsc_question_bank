### Question 4
**1] H1N1 virus is sometimes mentioned in the news with reference to which one of the following diseases? [2015]
**

a) AIDS

b) Bird flu

c) Dengue

d) Swine flu

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Swine flu

**Explanation:**
The H1N1 virus, often termed as swine flu, garners media attention due to its potential to cause widespread illness among humans. Originating in pigs, H1N1 is a subtype of the influenza A virus. When transmitted to humans, usually through close contact with infected pigs or their environments, it can result in respiratory illness. Swine flu symptoms often resemble those of seasonal flu, including fever, cough, sore throat, body aches, fatigue, and sometimes vomiting and diarrhea.

The H1N1 virus gained significant global attention during the 2009 influenza pandemic, where it spread rapidly across the world, causing widespread illness and fatalities. Although not as severe as initially feared, it highlighted the potential threat posed by novel influenza strains originating from animal reservoirs.

Effective surveillance, vaccination programs, and public health measures are crucial in managing and preventing outbreaks of swine flu. Regular monitoring and swift response to suspected cases help contain the spread of the virus and mitigate its impact on public health.
