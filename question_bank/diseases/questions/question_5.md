### Question 5
**1] Consider the following pairs: Vitamin Deficiency disease: [2014]**

1. Vitamin C: Scurvy
2. Vitamin D: Rickets
3. Vitamin E: Night blindness

Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 3 only

c) 1, 2 and 3

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1 and 2 only

**Explanation:**
The pairs 1 and 2 are correctly matched.

Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy, which is a disease characterized by bleeding gums, joint pain, and fatigue.

Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets, which is a disease that affects bone development in children and leads to soft, weak bones.

However, pair 3 is not correctly matched. Vitamin E deficiency does not cause night blindness. Vitamin A deficiency is responsible for causing night blindness.

Therefore, the correct pairing is:

1. Vitamin C : Scurvy

2. Vitamin D : Rickets

3. Vitamin A : Night blindness

Hence, the answer is “1 and 2 only”.
