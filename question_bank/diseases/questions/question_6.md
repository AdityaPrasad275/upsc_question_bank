### Question 6
**2] Consider the following diseases: [2014]
**
1. Diphtheria
2. Chickenpox
3. Smallpox

Which of the above diseases has/have been eradicated in India?

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 3 only

c) 1, 2 and 3

d) None

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 3 only

**Explanation:**
Eradication of Diseases in India

Introduction:

India has made significant progress in eradicating several deadly diseases over the years. Let’s discuss which of the given diseases have been eradicated in India.

Diphtheria:
– Diphtheria is a bacterial infection that affects the nose and throat.
– India has not yet eradicated diphtheria.
– However, the country has made remarkable progress in reducing the number of cases.

Chickenpox:
– Chickenpox is a highly contagious viral infection that causes a blistering rash and fever.
– India has not yet eradicated chickenpox.
– Although the disease is not life-threatening, it is still a significant public health concern.

Smallpox:
– Smallpox is a highly infectious disease caused by the variola virus.
– India has successfully eradicated smallpox since 1977.
– The country was declared free of smallpox by the World Health Organization (WHO) in 1980.

Conclusion:
– In conclusion, only smallpox has been eradicated in India.
– The country continues to work towards eradicating other deadly diseases through immunization programs, awareness campaigns, and better healthcare facilities.
