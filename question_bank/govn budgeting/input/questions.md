- **Statement I: Correct** – Revenue deficit = Revenue expenditure – Revenue receipts = 80,000 – 60,000 = ₹20,000 crores.

a) 1 only

**Explanation:**

So, both statements accurately describe aspects of the Indian economy’s fiscal dynamics, particularly in terms of government borrowing and debt management.
