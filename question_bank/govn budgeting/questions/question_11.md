### Question 11
**2] Which of the following is/are included in the capital budget of the Government of India? [2016]**

1. Expenditure on acquisition of assets like roads, buildings, machinery, etc.
2. Loans received from foreign governments
3. Loans and advances granted to the States and Union Territories
Select the correct answer using the code given below.


a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
**Capital Expenditure:**

- **1. Expenditure on acquisition of assets:** This includes costs associated with buying land, buildings, machinery, equipment, etc., that create long-term benefits for the country.

**Capital Receipts:**

- **2. Loans received from foreign governments:** These loans are a source of funding for capital expenditures. The government needs to repay them with interest.

- **3. Loans and advances granted to States and Union Territories:** While these are technically loans provided by the central government, they are considered part of capital receipts within the budget.

Therefore, all three options contribute to the capital budget of the Indian government, with options 1 and 3 being expenditures and option 2 being a source of funding (receipt)
