### Question 13
**2] There has been a persistent deficit budget year after year. Which of the following actions can be taken by the government to reduce the deficit? [2015]**

1. Reducing revenue expenditure
2. Introducing new welfare schemes
3. Rationalizing subsidies
4. Expanding industries
Select the correct answer using the code given below.


a) 1 and 3 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 only

d) 1,2,3 and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- **Reducing revenue expenditure**: This is a viable action to reduce the deficit. By cutting down on unnecessary or non-essential spending, the government can lower its overall expenditure and potentially reduce the deficit.

- **Introducing new welfare schemes**: Introducing new welfare schemes typically increases expenditure rather than reducing it. So, this action is unlikely to directly reduce the deficit.

- **Rationalizing subsidies**: Rationalizing subsidies involves reevaluating and possibly reducing subsidies on various goods and services. This action can help reduce expenditure and thereby contribute to deficit reduction.

- **Expanding industries**: While expanding industries can potentially increase government revenue through taxes and create jobs, it may not directly reduce the deficit in the short term. It’s more of a long-term economic strategy rather than an immediate deficit reduction measure.

Given this analysis, the actions that could be taken by the government to reduce the deficit are:

- **Reducing revenue expenditure**

- **Rationalizing subsidies**
