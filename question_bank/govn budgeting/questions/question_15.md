### Question 15
**1] In India deficit financing is used for raising resources for? [2013]

**
a) Economic development

b) Redemption of public debt

c) Adjusting the balance payments

d) Reducing the foreign debt

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Economic development

**Explanation:**
- **Deficit financing** involves the government spending more than it collects in revenue. This gap is bridged by borrowing money or printing new currency.

The primary purpose of deficit financing in India is to **fund investments in economic development**. This can include:

- Building infrastructure projects like roads, bridges, and dams.

- Investing in social programs like education and healthcare.

- Stimulating specific sectors of the economy.

While deficit financing might have some impact on other aspects like the balance of payments, its primary goal is to promote economic growth and development.
