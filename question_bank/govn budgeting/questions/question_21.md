### Question 21
**5] All revenues received by the union government by way of taxes and other receipts for the conduct of government business are credited to the? [2011]**


a) Contingency fund of India.

b) Public account.

c) Consolidated fund of India

d) Deposits and advances fund.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Consolidated fund of India

**Explanation:**
a) **Contingency fund of India**: The Contingency Fund of India is a fund established by the Constitution of India to meet unforeseen expenditure by the government. It is held by the President of India and does not include revenues received by the government.


b) **Public account**: The Public Account of India is used for transactions relating to debt and other liabilities, deposits, and advances. It does not include revenues received by the government for the conduct of government business.


c) **Consolidated fund of India**: As mentioned earlier, the Consolidated Fund of India is the main account of the central government where all revenues received by the government, including taxes and other receipts, are credited. It is used to meet all expenses of the government.


d) **Deposits and advances fund**: This option does not correspond to a specific fund in the context of the central government’s finances.
