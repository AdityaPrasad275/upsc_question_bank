### Question 24
**2] In the context of governance, consider the following: [2010]**

1. Encouraging Foreign Direct Investment inflows.
2. Privatization of higher educational Institutions.
3. Down – sizing of bureaucracy.
4. Selling / offloading the shares of Public Sector Undertakings.

Which of the above can be used as measures to control the fiscal deficit in India?

a) 1, 2 and 3 
b) 2, 3 and 4

c) 1, 2 and 4 
d) 3 and 4 only

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2, 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
- **Encouraging Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) inflows:** This might not directly affect the deficit in the short term. While FDI can potentially lead to increased economic activity and tax revenue in the long run, it may not have an immediate impact on government spending or revenue collection.

- **Privatization of Higher Educational Institutions:** Government does incur expenses in maintaining educational institutions. Thus, privatization of same can potentially lead to reduced government expenditure and in turn control the fiscal deficit.

- **Downsizing of Bureaucracy:** This can lead to a reduction in government expenditure on salaries and pensions for bureaucrats. This is a direct and significant way to reduce the deficit.

- **Selling Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) Shares:** Similar to privatization, selling PSU shares can generate revenue for the government, thereby helping to reduce the fiscal deficit.
