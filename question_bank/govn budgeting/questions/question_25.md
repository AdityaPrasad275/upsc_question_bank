### Question 25
**3] Which one of the following is responsible for the preparation and presentation of Union Budget to the Parliament? [2010]**


a) Department of Revenue

b) Department of Economic Affairs

c) Department of Financial Services

d) Department of Expenditure

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) Department of Economic Affairs

**Explanation:**
The Department of Economic Affairs (DE
A) is one of the key departments under the Ministry of Finance in the Government of India. Its primary responsibility is to formulate and implement economic policies and programs of the Government of India. One of the significant tasks of the DEA is the preparation and presentation of the Union Budget to the Parliament of India.

The Union Budget, also known as the Annual Financial Statement, is a comprehensive document that outlines the government’s revenue and expenditure for the upcoming financial year. It reflects the government’s fiscal policies, priorities, and plans for economic development. The DEA plays a central role in coordinating with other departments and agencies to compile budgetary proposals, analyze economic data, and draft the budget document.

During the budget-making process, the DEA works closely with various departments, including the Department of Revenue, Department of Expenditure, and other relevant ministries, to ensure that all aspects of government finances are appropriately considered and reflected in the budget. Once the budget is finalized, the DEA presents it to the Parliament for approval and subsequent implementation.

Overall, the DEA’s involvement in the budgetary process underscores its crucial role in shaping India’s economic policies and ensuring fiscal discipline and transparency in government finances.
