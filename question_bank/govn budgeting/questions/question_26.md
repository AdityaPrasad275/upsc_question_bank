### Question 26
**4] Consider the following actions by the Government: [2010]**

1. Cutting the tax rates
2. Increasing the government spending
3. Abolishing the subsidies

In the context of economic recession, which of the above actions can be considered a part of the “fiscal stimulus” package?

a) 1 and 2 only 
b) 2 only

c) 1 and 3 only 
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1 and 2 only

**Explanation:**
Cutting the tax rates: Reducing tax rates can stimulate economic activity by putting more money in the hands of consumers and businesses, thereby increasing spending and investment. This is a common fiscal stimulus measure aimed at stimulating demand.
Increasing government spending: Increasing government spending on infrastructure projects, social programs, and other initiatives can directly boost aggregate demand in the economy, create jobs, and support economic growth. This is a key component of fiscal stimulus packages.
Abolishing subsidies: Abolishing subsidies typically involves reducing government spending rather than increasing it. While cutting inefficient subsidies can improve fiscal sustainability, it may not directly stimulate economic activity during a recession. In fact, in certain cases, targeted subsidies may be used as a form of fiscal stimulus to support specific sectors or segments of the economy.
