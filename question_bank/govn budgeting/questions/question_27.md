### Question 27
**5] Which one of the following was not stipulated in the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act , 2003? [2010]**


a) Elimination of revenue deficit by the end of the Fiscal year 2007-2008.

b) Non borrowing by the central government from Reserve Bank of India except under certain Circumstance.

c) Elimination of Primary deficit by the end of the fiscal year 2008-2009.

d) Fixing government Guarantees in any financial year as a percentage of GDP.

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Elimination of Primary deficit by the end of the fiscal year 2008-2009.

**Explanation:**
- The Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act (FRBM), 2003 aimed to achieve fiscal consolidation in India by setting targets for deficit reduction.

The Act indeed included the following provisions:

- **Elimination of revenue deficit:** This refers to a situation where government revenue covers its non-debt expenditures. The FRBM Act targeted eliminating the revenue deficit by a specific year.

- **Non-borrowing from RBI except under special circumstances:** The Act aimed to limit government borrowing from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to control inflation and promote responsible fiscal management.

- **Fixing government guarantees:** The Act aimed to limit government guarantees provided to loans or other financial instruments, as these can also create potential liabilities.

- The FRBM Act did not explicitly target the elimination of the primary deficit by a specific date.
