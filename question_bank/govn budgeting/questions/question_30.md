### Question 30
**8] Consider the following statements: [2004]**

1. Reserve Bank of India was nationalised on 26 January, 1950.
2. The borrowing programme of the Government of India is handled by the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Explanation:**
- **Reserve Bank of India was nationalized on 26 January 1950**: This statement is incorrect. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was not nationalized on 26 January 1950. The RBI was established in 1935 and was initially privately owned with a significant share of ownership held by private shareholders. It was later nationalized on 1 January 1949.

- **The borrowing program of the Government of India is handled by the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance**: This statement is incorrect. The borrowing program of the Government of India is handled by the Department of Economic Affairs (DE
A), not the Department of Expenditure. The DEA is one of the departments under the Ministry of Finance responsible for formulating and implementing economic policies related to public debt management, external financing, and fiscal policy.
