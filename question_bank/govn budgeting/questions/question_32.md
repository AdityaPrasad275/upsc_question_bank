### Question 32
**10] Which one of the following authorities recommends the principles governing the grants- in-aid of the revenues to the states out of the Consolidated Fund of India? [2002]**


a) Finance Commission

b) Inter-State Council

c) Union Ministry of Finance

d) Public Accounts Committee

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) Finance Commission

**Explanation:**
- The Finance Commission of India plays a crucial role in recommending principles for distributing resources from the central government to states. This includes grants-in-aid provided to states from the Consolidated Fund of India.

The Finance Commission is a constitutional body established under Article 280 of the Indian Constitution. It is tasked with:

- Recommending principles for the distribution of central taxes between the Union and the States.

- Recommending principles for determining the grants-in-aid of the revenues of the States out of the Consolidated Fund of India.

- Examining the financial position of the Union and the States and suggesting measures to improve their financial position.
