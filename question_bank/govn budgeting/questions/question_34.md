### Question 34
**12]** **In the year 2001, Germany approved a $32 million credit to India: [2002]**


a) To promote primary education in selected states

b) For Tehri dam project

c) To assist in the development of nuclear

d) For oceanographic research

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) For oceanographic research

**Explanation:**
Germany’s approval of a $32 million credit to India for oceanographic research reflects a collaborative effort between the two countries in the field of marine science and research. Oceanographic research involves the study of the oceans, including their physical, chemical, biological, and geological characteristics, as well as the exploration of marine resources and ecosystems.

This funding likely aimed to support various aspects of oceanographic research activities in India, such as:

- **Marine exploration**: Funding could be used for expeditions and surveys to study marine environments, including coastal areas, deep-sea regions, and marine biodiversity.

- **Data collection and analysis**: Resources may be allocated for the acquisition of oceanographic data through instruments like buoys, sensors, and research vessels, as well as for data analysis and interpretation.

- **Technology development**: Funding could support the development and deployment of new technologies and instrumentation for oceanographic research, including remote sensing, autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs), and oceanographic modeling tools.

- **Capacity building**: Investments may be directed towards training programs, workshops, and research collaborations aimed at building the capacity of Indian scientists and institutions in the field of oceanography.
