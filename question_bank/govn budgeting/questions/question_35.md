### Question 35
**13] Consider the following: [2001]**

I. Market borrowing
II. Treasury bills
III. Special securities issued to RBI

Which of these is/are component(s) of internal debt?

a) I only 
b) I and II 

c) II only 
d) I, II and III

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) I, II and III

**Explanation:**
I. **Market borrowing**: Market borrowing involves the government raising funds by issuing securities such as bonds and debentures to investors in the open market. These securities are sold to domestic institutions, banks, and individuals.

II. **Treasury bills**: Treasury bills (T-bills) are short-term debt instruments issued by the government to raise funds for short periods, usually less than one year. They are typically sold at a discount to their face value and redeemed at face value upon maturity.

III. **Special securities issued to RBI**: Special securities issued to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) represent borrowings by the government from the central bank. These securities may be issued for various purposes, including liquidity management and monetary policy operations.

Based on the definitions provided, all three components—market borrowing, treasury bills, and special securities issued to RBI—are components of internal debt.
