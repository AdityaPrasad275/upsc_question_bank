### Question 5
**1] With reference to the Indian economy, consider the following statements: [2022]**

1. A share of the household financial savings goes towards government borrowings.
2. Dated securities issued at market-related rates in auctions form a large component of internal debt.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?


a) 1 only

b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2

d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- **A share of the household financial savings goes towards government borrowings**: This statement is correct. In India, households invest their savings in various financial instruments, including government securities. These savings indirectly contribute to government borrowings by purchasing government bonds, treasury bills, and other debt instruments issued by the government.

- **Dated securities issued at market-related rates in auctions form a large component of internal debt**: This statement is also correct. Dated securities are long-term debt instruments issued by the government through auctions. These securities have fixed interest rates and maturity dates. The government regularly conducts auctions to issue dated securities at market-related rates to finance its fiscal deficit and meet other expenditure requirements. These securities constitute a significant portion of India’s internal debt, which is the debt owed by the government to domestic creditors.

So, both statements accurately describe aspects of the Indian economy’s fiscal dynamics, particularly in terms of government borrowing and debt management.
