### Question 6
**1] Along with the Budget, the Finance Minister also places other documents before the Parliament which include ‘The Macro Economic Framework Statement’. The aforesaid document is presented because this is mandated by: [2020]

**
a) Long standing parliamentary convention

b) Article 112 and Article 110(1) of the Constitution of India

c) Article 113 of the Constitution of India

d) Provisions of the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Provisions of the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003

**Explanation:**
**Explanation:**

- The FRBM Act is a legislation that aims to ensure responsible fiscal management by the government.

- It mandates the government to present the Macroeconomic Framework Statement alongside the budget.

This statement provides an assessment of the following:

- Growth prospects of the Indian economy

- Underlying assumptions used in preparing the budget

- Fiscal targets for the government

Presenting this statement promotes transparency and allows for better evaluation of the government’s budget proposals.
