### Question 8
**1] Consider the following statements: [2019]**

1. Most of India’s external debt is owed by governmental entities.
2. All of India’s external debt is denominated in US dollars.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?


a) 1 only

b) 2 only

c) Both 1 and 2

d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Explanation:**
**Governmental vs. Non-governmental Debt:** There isn’t a definitive statement about the majority holder of India’s external debt. It can be owed by:

- The Indian government (central and state)

- Indian corporations

- Indian individuals

While the government may be a significant borrower, it’s not necessarily the sole or even the majority holder.

**Currency Denomination of Debt:** External debt can be denominated in various currencies, not just US dollars. While USD might be a major currency, other options include:

- Euro

- Japanese Yen

- Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) issued by the International Monetary Fund (IMF)

So, both statements have limitations.
