**1] With reference to radioisotope thermoelectric generators (RTGs), consider the following statements: [2024]

**1. RTGs are miniature fission reactors.
2. RTGs are used for powering the onboard systems of spacecrafts.
3. RTGs can use Plutonium-238, which is a by-product of weapons development.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 2 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
- Radioisotope thermoelectric generators (RTGs) provide electrical power to spacecraft **using heat from the natural radioactive decay of plutonium-238, in the form of plutonium oxide.** Thus, first statement is wrong.

- Electrical power for Cassini spacecraft’s science instruments and onboard systems was generated by three RTGs. (Reference). Thus, second statement is correct.

- Pure plutonium-238 is prepared by neutron irradiation of neptunium-237, one of the minor **actinides that can be recovered from spent nuclear fuel during reprocessing**, **or by the neutron irradiation of americium in a reactor. **Thus, it appears that Pu-238 can be obtained as a by-product of weapons development, making third statement true.

- Thus, 
b) 2 and 3 is correct answer.
