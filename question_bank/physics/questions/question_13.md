### Question 13
**5] The efforts to detect the existence of Higgs boson particle have become frequent news in the recent past. What is/ are the importance of discovering this particle? [2013]
**
1. It will enable us to under-stand as to why elementary particles have mass.
2. It will enable us in the near future to develop the technology of transferring matter from one point to another without traversing the physical space between them.
3. It will enable us to create better fuels for nuclear fission.

Select the correct answer using the codes given below.

a) 1 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
a) 1 only

**Explanation:**
- **It will enable us to understand why elementary particles have mass**: This statement is correct. The discovery of the Higgs boson confirms the existence of the Higgs field, which interacts with particles and gives them mass through the process of electroweak symmetry breaking. Understanding this mechanism is crucial for comprehending the origin of mass in the universe.

- **It will enable us in the near future to develop the technology of transferring matter from one point to another without traversing the physical space between them**: This statement is incorrect. The discovery of the Higgs boson does not directly relate to the development of matter transfer technology or concepts like teleportation, which are currently speculative and theoretical rather than based on known particle physics principles.

- **It will enable us to create better fuels for nuclear fission**: This statement is incorrect. The discovery of the Higgs boson does not directly impact the development of nuclear fuels or nuclear fission technology.
