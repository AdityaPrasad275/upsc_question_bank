### Question 19
**3]** **A new optical disc format known as the blu-ray disc (B
D) is becoming popular. In what way is it different from the traditional DVD? [2011]**

1. DVD supports standard definition video while BD supports high definition video.
2. Compared to a DVD, the BD format has several times more storage capacity.
3. Thickness of BD is 2.4 mm while that of DVD is 1.2 mm.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a) 1 only 
b) 1 and 2 only

c) 2 and 3 only 
d) 1,2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
b) 1 and 2 only

**Explanation:**
**The Difference between DVD and Blu-ray Disc (B
D)**

The question asks about the differences between the traditional DVD and the new optical disc format known as the Blu-ray Disc (B
D).

The correct statements are:
1. DVD supports standard definition video while BD supports high definition video.
2. Compared to a DVD, the BD format has several times more storage capacity.

Explanation:

1. Video Quality
– DVD supports standard definition video with a resolution of 720×480 pixels.
– BD supports high definition video with a resolution of 1920×1080 pixels or even higher.

2. Storage Capacity
– A single-layer DVD can store up to 4.7 GB of data.
– A single-layer BD can store up to 25 GB of data, and a dual-layer BD can store up to 50 GB of data. This means that BD has several times more storage capacity than DVD.

3. Thickness
– The thickness of a BD is 2.4 mm, while the thickness of a DVD is 1.2 mm. This difference in thickness is due to the different technologies used to read and write data on the disc.

Conclusion: In summary, the Blu-ray Disc (B
D) format is different from the traditional DVD in terms of video quality, storage capacity, and thickness. BD supports high definition video, has several times more storage capacity, and is thicker than DVD.
