### Question 5
**2] In a pressure cooker, the temperature at which the food is cooked depends mainly upon which of the following? [2021]
**
1) Area of the hole in the lid
2) Temperature of the flame
3) Weight of the lid

Select the correct answer using the code given below.

a) 1 and 2 only

b) 2 and 3 only

c) 1 and 3 only

d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) 1 and 3 only

**Explanation:**
**Area of the hole in the lid**:

- **Correctness**: This statement is correct. The size of the hole in the pressure cooker lid does influence the rate at which steam is released from the cooker. A larger hole allows steam to escape more quickly, potentially reducing the pressure inside the cooker and affecting the cooking temperature. Conversely, a smaller hole restricts the escape of steam, maintaining higher pressure and temperature within the cooker. So, it indirectly affects the cooking temperature by regulating pressure.

**Temperature of the flame**:

- **Correctness**: This statement is incorrect. While the flame’s temperature affects the overall heating of the pressure cooker, it doesn’t directly regulate the internal pressure or cooking temperature. The flame’s temperature primarily influences how quickly the pressure cooker reaches its desired operating pressure, but once there, the pressure inside and thus the cooking temperature are primarily determined by other factors like the weight of the lid and the heat transfer efficiency.

**Weight of the lid**:

- **Correctness**: This statement is correct. The weight of the lid directly affects the pressure inside the pressure cooker. A heavier lid creates more pressure, raising the boiling point of water and consequently increasing the cooking temperature. Therefore, the weight of the lid is a crucial factor in determining the cooking temperature inside the pressure cooker.
