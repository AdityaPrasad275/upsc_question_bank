### Question 7
**1] India is an important member of the ‘International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor’. If this experiment succeeds, what is the immediate advantage for India? [2016]
**

a) It can use thorium in place of uranium for power generation

b) It can attain a global role in satellite navigation

c) It can drastically improve the efficiency of its fission reactors in power generation

d) It can build fusion reactors for power generation

**Answer:**
Ans: 
d) It can build fusion reactors for power generation

**Explanation:**
If the International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor (ITER) experiment succeeds, India stands to gain the immediate advantage of being able to build fusion reactors for power generation. Fusion reactors hold immense promise as they replicate the same process that powers the sun, fusing hydrogen isotopes to release vast amounts of energy. Unlike traditional fission reactors, which produce energy by splitting atoms, fusion reactors offer numerous benefits, including abundant fuel supply (primarily isotopes of hydrogen found in water), minimal radioactive waste, and significantly reduced risks of nuclear proliferation.

For India, the ability to develop fusion reactors would revolutionize its energy landscape. Fusion reactors could provide a nearly limitless and clean source of energy, reducing dependence on fossil fuels and mitigating environmental impacts such as greenhouse gas emissions. Additionally, fusion technology offers the potential to address India’s growing energy demand, enhance energy security, and propel advancements in science and technology.

Furthermore, mastery of fusion technology could position India as a global leader in the field, fostering international collaborations and economic opportunities. Thus, the success of the ITER experiment holds the key to unlocking India’s path towards sustainable and abundant energy through fusion power generation.
