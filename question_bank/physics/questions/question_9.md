### Question 9
**1] Ball bearings are used in bicycles, cars, etc., because__: [2013]
**

a) The actual area of contact between the wheel and axle is increased

b) The effective area of contact between the wheel and axle is increased

c) The effective area of contact between the wheel and axle is reduced

d) None of the statements is correct

**Answer:**
Ans: 
c) The effective area of contact between the wheel and axle is reduced

**Explanation:**
Ball bearings work by using balls that roll between the inner and outer races of the bearing. This rolling motion creates less friction compared to a sliding motion between the wheel and axle. Friction is the force that resists relative motion between two surfaces in contact.

Here’s why the other options are incorrect:

- **
a) Incorrect:** The actual area of contact between the wheel and axle might be slightly increased due to the presence of the balls in the bearing, but this is not the primary benefit.

- **
b) Incorrect:** As mentioned earlier, ball bearings reduce friction, and friction is directly proportional to the effective area of contact. So, reducing the contact area helps minimize friction.

- **
d) Incorrect:** Ball bearings are a widely used and essential component due to their ability to reduce friction.

By reducing the effective area of contact and utilizing rolling motion, ball bearings significantly decrease friction between the wheel and axle. This allows for smoother and more efficient movement of the wheels.
