### Question
**5] Consider the following communication technologies: [2022]**

1. Closed-circuit Television
2. Radio Frequency Identification
3. Wireless Local Area Network

Which, of the above are considered Short-Range devices/technologies?
a) 1 and 2 only
b) 2 and 3 only
c) 1 and 3 only
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2 and 3

**Explanation:**
- Short-range technologies enable communication over a limited distance, usually within a few meters to a few hundred meters. The examples of this include Bluetooth, WIFI, NFC (Near Field Communication), RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) etc.



- On the other hand, long-range technologies enable communication over long distances, from several kilometers to hundreds or thousands of kilometers. The examples of this include cellular networks (3G, 4G, 5G etc.), Satellite Communication, TV Broadcast etc. 



- Thus, all the three technologies given above are examples of short range communication.
