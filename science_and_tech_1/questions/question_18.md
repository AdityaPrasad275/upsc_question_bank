### Question
**1] With reference to communication technologies, what is/are the difference/differences between LTE (Long-Term Evolution) and VoLTE (Voice over Long-Term Evolution)? [2019]**

1. LTE is commonly marketed as 3G and VoLTE is commonly marketed as advanced 3G.
2. LTE is data-only technology and VoLTE is voice-only technology.

Select the correct answer using the code given below.
a) 1 only
b) 2 only
c) Both 1 and 2
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Explanation:**
- **LTE (Long-Term Evolution):**



- LTE is a standard for wireless broadband communication that provides high-speed data transmission for mobile devices. The technology focuses on delivering high-speed internet access, allowing users to browse the web, stream videos, download files, and access other data-intensive applications.



- LTE networks are optimized for data transmission and may not natively support voice calls over the LTE network itself.



- **VoLTE (Voice over Long-Term Evolution)**



- VoLTE, on the other hand, is an extension of LTE technology that enables voice calls to be made over LTE networks.



- It allows voice calls to be transmitted as data packets over LTE networks, providing higher quality voice calls compared to traditional circuit-switched voice calls.



- VoLTE enables features such as HD voice, faster call setup times, and simultaneous voice and data transmission.



- With VoLTE, voice calls are treated as a type of data transmission, similar to other data services on LTE networks.



- In summary, LTE focuses on high-speed data transmission, while VoLTE specifically enables voice calls to be made over LTE networks by transmitting voice as data packets.



- **LTE technology was introduced as part of the transition from 3G to 4G, offering faster data speeds and improved network capacity.**



- **Thus, both the statements are incorrect.**
