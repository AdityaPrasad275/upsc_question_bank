### Question
**5] When the alarm of your smartphone rings in the morning, you wake up and tap it to stop the alarm which causes your geyser to be switched on automatically. The smart mirror in your bathroom shows the day’s weather and also indicates the level of water in your overhead tank. After you take some groceries from your refrigerator for making breakfast, it recognises the shortage of stock in it and places and order for the supply of fresh grocery items. When you step out of your house and lock the door, all lights, fans, geysers and AC machines get switched off automatically. On your way to office, your car warns you about traffic congestion ahead and suggests an alternative route, and if you are late for a meeting, it sends a message to your office accordingly. In the context of emerging communication technologies, which one of the following terms best applies to the above scenario? [2018]**

a) Border Gateway Protocol
b) Internet Of Things
c) Internet Protocol
d) Visual Private Network

**Answer:**
Ans: b) Internet Of Things

**Explanation:**
- **a) Border Gateway Protocol (BGP)** is a standardized exterior gateway protocol designed to exchange routing information between different autonomous systems on the internet. It is used by internet service providers (ISPs) and large organizations to route traffic between networks and ensure efficient data transmission.



- **b) Internet of Things (IoT) **refers to a network of interconnected devices or technologies, embedded with sensors, software etc. that enable them to collect and exchange data with each other over the internet. This technology enables a wide range of applications across various industries, including smart homes, healthcare, transportation, agriculture, and industrial automation etc. **The above explained scenario refers to an illustration of what all things that are possible with IoT.**



- **c) Internet Protocol (IP)**, is a set of rules governing the format and addressing scheme used to route data packets across networks, including the internet. It provides the framework for communication between devices. IPv4 and IPv6 are the two main versions of the Internet Protocol currently in use.



- **d) Virtual Private Network (VPN)** is a secure and encrypted connection established over a public network, typically the internet, to provide users with privacy, anonymity, and security while accessing the internet. VPNs are commonly used to protect online privacy, bypass censorship or geo-restrictions, and secure remote access to corporate networks.
