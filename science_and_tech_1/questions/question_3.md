### Question
**3] ****Consider the following statements:** **[2025/A-81]

**Statement I: Some rare earth elements are used in the manufacture of flat television screens and computer monitors.
Statement II: Some rare earth elements have phosphorescent properties.
Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

a) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct and Statement II explains Statement I
b) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct but Statement II does not explain Statement I
c) Statement I is correct but Statement II is not correct
d) Statement I is not correct but Statement II is correct

**Answer:**
Ans: a) **&nbsp;**Both Statement I and Statement II are correct and Statement II explains Statement I

**Explanation:**
- **Statement I: Correct** – Rare earth elements such as europium, terbium, and yttrium are used in making flat TV screens and computer monitors.



- **Statement II: Correct** – These elements possess phosphorescent properties, allowing them to emit light when excited, which explains their use in display technologies.



- Hence, both statements are correct, and Statement II explains Statement I
