### Question
**1] With reference to ‘Li-Fi’, recently in the news, which of the following statements is/are correct? [2016]**

1. It uses light as the medium for high-speed data transmission.
2. It is a wireless technology and is several times faster than ‘WiFi’.

Select the correct answer using the code given below.
a) 1 only
b) 2 only
c) Both 1 and 2
d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Answer:**
Ans: c) Both 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- **Li-Fi, short for Light Fidelity**, is a wireless communication technology that uses light to transmit data. Li-Fi utilizes visible light from LED bulbs to transmit data. The LED bulbs modulate their light intensity at extremely high speeds, imperceptible to the human eye, to encode data. A photodetector on the receiving device captures the light signals and decodes them into electronic data.



- Li-Fi can achieve **very high data transfer rates**, potentially exceeding those of traditional Wi-Fi, due to the large bandwidth of visible light.



- Unlike radio frequency (RF) communications like Wi-Fi, Li-Fi is not affected by electromagnetic interference, making it suitable for use in environments where RF interference can be an issue, such as hospitals and aircraft.



- Further, since light cannot penetrate walls, Li-Fi provides a higher level of security for local data transmission compared to Wi-Fi, which can extend beyond physical barriers.



- This technology has potential applications in areas like smart lighting, internet of things (IoT), indoor navigation, and in environments where RF communication is restricted or undesirable.



- **Thus, both the statements given in the question are correct**
