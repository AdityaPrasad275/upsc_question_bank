### Question
**1] What is the difference between blue-tooth and Wi-Fi devices? [2011]**

a) Bluetooth uses 2.4 GHz radio frequency band, whereas Wi-Fi can use 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz frequency band.
b) Bluetooth is used for wireless local area networks (WLAN) only, whereas Wi-Fi&nbsp; is used for wireless wide area networks (WWAN) only.
c) When information is transmitted between two devices using blue-tooth technology , the devices have to be in the line of sight of each other, but when Wi-Fi technology is used the devices need not be in the line of sight of each other.
d) The statements (a) and (b) given above are correct in this context.

**Answer:**
Ans: a) Bluetooth uses 2.4 GHz radio frequency band, whereas Wi-Fi can use 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz frequency band.
b) Bluetooth is used for wireless local area networks (WLAN) only, whereas Wi-Fi&nbsp; is used for wireless wide area networks (WWAN) only.

**Explanation:**
- Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are both wireless communication technologies used for transmitting data between devices without the need for physical connections.



- Both technologies use radio waves to transmit data wirelessly and are widely used for connecting devices to each other and to networks.



- Bluetooth operates in the 2.4 GHz ISM (Industrial, Scientific, and Medical) band. While Wi-Fi Operates in the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands. (6 GHz band was introduced with Wi-Fi 6E, and may not be relevant in 2011 – when question was asked).



- There is no restriction regarding using either technology in WLAN or WWAN. However, Bluetooth will not be practical to be used in WWAN.



- Both technologies do not need devices to be in line of sight of each other.



- **Thus, only 1st statement is correct.**
