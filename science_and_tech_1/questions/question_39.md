### Question
**5] What does the term Dolby B, or Dolby C. printed on tape recorders and other sound system refer to? [2006]**

a) Frequency modulated system
b) Amplitude Modulated system
c) Noise reduction circuit
d) Both DC and AC power can be used

**Answer:**
Ans: c) Noise reduction circuit

**Explanation:**
- The terms Dolby B and Dolby C refer to **noise reduction systems** used in tape recorders and other sound systems to reduce the background hiss that is inherent in analogue recordings. These systems work by boosting high-frequency signals during recording and reducing them during playback, which lowers the noise level.
