### Question
**6] Consider the following statements: [2004]**

1. Smart Card is a plastic card with an embedded microchip.
2. Digital technology is primarily used with new physical communication medium such as satellite and fibre optics transmission.
3. A digital library is a collection of documents in an organised electronic form available on the Internet only.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
a) 3 only&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; b) 1 and 2
c) 2 and 3&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: b) 1 and 2

**Explanation:**
- **A smart card** is a small plastic card embedded with an integrated circuit chip. These chips can store and process data securely. Smart cards are used in various applications for identification, authentication, data storage, and financial transactions. Smart cards can be found in credit cards, identity cards, SIM cards for mobile phones, access control cards, and more.



- **Digital technology** is commonly associated with modern physical communication mediums such as satellite and fibre optics transmission.



- **A digital library **is a collection of digital resources, such as books, articles, images, audio files, and videos, that are stored electronically and accessible via computers or other digital devices. These resources allow users to search, browse, and retrieve information efficiently**. However, it is not necessary that a digital library is accessible only over internet. It can be used offline as well.**



- **Hence b is the answer**
