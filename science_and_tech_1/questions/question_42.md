### Question
**8] The world’s fastest computer has been able to perform (as of Dec. 1996): [1997]**

a) 106 operations per second
b) 109 operations per second
c) 1012 operations per second
d) 1015 operations per second

**Answer:**
Ans: c) 1012 operations per second

**Explanation:**
- As of December 1996, the world’s fastest computer was able to perform approximately 1 trillion floating-point operations per second (1 teraflop). This computer was the ASCI Red supercomputer, developed by Intel and installed at the Sandia National Laboratories in the United States. It was the first computer to achieve sustained performance at the teraflop level.



- As of June 2024, the number one supercomputer is Frontier and has a maximum speed of 1.1 exaflops or 1.1 x 1018 &nbsp;operations per second.
