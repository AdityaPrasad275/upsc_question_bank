### Question
**1] With reference to Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs), consider the following statements: [2022]**

1. They enable the digital representation of physical assets.
2. They are unique cryptographic tokens that exist on a blockchain.
3. They can be traded or exchanged at equivalency and therefore can be used as a medium of commercial transactions.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
a) 1 and 2 only
b) 2 and 3 only
c) 1 and 3 only
d) 1, 2 and 3

**Answer:**
Ans: a) 1 and 2 only

**Explanation:**
- An NFT, or Non-Fungible Token, is a type of **digital asset** that represents ownership or proof of authenticity of a unique item or piece of content, usually stored on a blockchain. 



- NFTs can represent various forms of digital or physical assets, including digital artwork, animations, images, audio files, albums, or even parcels of land in virtual worlds and online games.



- While NFTs don’t always represent physical asset, it definitely enables the digital representation of physical asset.  **So the statement 1 is true.** 



- 2nd statement is also true since NFTs use blockchain technology and are created using cryptographic technology, ensuring their security and authenticity.** Thus, statement 2 is also true. **



- **The third statement is false.** NFTs are unique and non-fungible, meaning each one has a distinct value and cannot be exchanged on a one-to-one basis with another NFT. This is different from fungible tokens like cryptocurrencies (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum), which can be traded or exchanged at equivalency.



- Because of their unique properties and varying values, NFTs are not used as a general medium of exchange in commercial transactions. Instead, they are often bought and sold in specialized marketplaces where their unique attributes and provenance determine their value.
