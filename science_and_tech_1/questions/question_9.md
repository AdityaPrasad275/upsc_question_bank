### Question
**2] Consider the following: [2022]**

1. Aarogya Setu
2. CoWIN
3. DigiLocker
4. DIKSHA

Which of the above are built on top of open-source digital platforms?
a) 1 and 2 only
b) 2, 3 and 4 only
c) 1, 3 and 4 only
d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Answer:**
Ans: d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Explanation:**
- An open-source platform is a software system where the source code is made freely available to everyone. This allows anyone to see, use, modify, and distribute the code as they wish.



- All the platforms mentioned above were built on top on open source digital platforms. 











- **Aarogya Setu** is a mobile application developed by the Government of India to help in the fight against COVID-19. The app aims to track and alert users about their risk of exposure to COVID-19 by using Bluetooth and GPS to determine if they have been in close contact with someone who has tested positive for the virus. 



- Initially, Aarogya Setu was not open-source, which raised concerns about transparency and security.



- In May 2020, the government released the source code of the Aarogya Setu app, making it open-source. This move allowed developers and security experts to examine the code, identify vulnerabilities, and suggest improvements, enhancing the app’s transparency and trustworthiness











- **CoWIN** (COVID Vaccine Intelligence Network) is an online platform developed by the Government of India to manage and streamline the COVID-19 vaccination drive across the country.



- The CoWIN platform’s API (Application Programming Interface) has been made open for integration with other applications to expand its reach and functionality, though the full source code is not open-source.











- **DigiLocker** is a cloud-based platform provided by the Government of India for the secure storage, sharing, and verification of documents and certificates.



- DigiLocker itself is not fully open-source, but it integrates with various open standards and APIs to ensure interoperability with other digital platforms and services.











- **DIKSHA** (Digital Infrastructure for Knowledge Sharing) is an initiative by the Government of India to provide a digital platform for teachers and students to access educational resources.



- DIKSHA is built on Sunbird, an open-source digital learning platform. This open-source nature allows for customization, scalability, and community contributions to the platform’s development.
